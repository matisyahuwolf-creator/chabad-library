# Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn — The Rebbe Rashab (1860–1920)

- **English name:** Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn
- **Hebrew name:** רבי שלום דובער שניאורסאהן
- **Honorific / acronym:** The Rebbe Rashab (רש"ב, from **R**eb **Sh**olom **B**er); also called *"the Rambam (Maimonides) of Chassidus"* for his systematic, encyclopedic style
- **Life years:** 20 Cheshvan 5621 – 2 Nisan 5680 (5 November 1860, Lyubavichi – 21 March 1920, Rostov-on-Don)
- **Generation:** 5th Rebbe of the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty
- **Years of leadership:** His father, the Rebbe Maharash, passed on 13 Tishrei 5643 (1882), after which the Rashab gradually assumed leadership; he formally accepted the mantle of Rebbe in 1893 and led until his passing in 1920. Chabad.org counts this as approximately **38 years** of leadership (from 1882); counting from formal acceptance (~1893) yields roughly 27 years.

---

The Rebbe Rashab was the fifth leader of Chabad-Lubavitch, son and successor of **Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn** (the Rebbe Maharash, fourth Rebbe) and father and predecessor of **Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn** (the Rebbe Rayatz/Frierdiker Rebbe, sixth Rebbe). He is best remembered for founding the **Tomchei Temimim** yeshiva network in Lubavitch (1897) — the institutional vehicle that preserved Chabad through the Soviet era — and for transforming the delivery of Chabad Chassidus into long, structured, multi-year discourse-cycles (*hemshechim*) written and edited with unprecedented rigor. His two monumental series, **Samach Vov** (5666) and **Ayin Beis** (5672), are studied to this day as the in-depth "oral-tradition" curriculum of Chabad. He also authored a body of practical *kuntreisim* (tracts) on prayer and divine service that became the avodah manual of the Temimim, and led a forceful rabbinic campaign against secular Zionism and the Haskalah. His teachings are noted for marshaling the entire prior Chabad corpus into orderly, comprehensible expositions accessible to advanced students and beginners alike.

## The Corpus at a Glance

The Rebbe Rashab's output is unusually large — by Chabad.org's account he **wrote and delivered some 2,000 chassidic discourses** over the thirty-eight years of his leadership (some secondary Chabad sources give a lower figure of ~1,500), filling close to **40 published volumes** of teachings, with the full corpus (counting the *Likkutim* compilations and the *Igros Kodesh*) cited as high as ~50 volumes. It divides into four main streams: (1) **Chassidus / Maamarim** — formal Chassidic discourses, his dominant genre, published as *Sefer HaMaamarim* organized year-by-year (the bulk of the corpus), including the two great multi-year *hemshechim*; (2) **Tracts (Kuntreisim)** — shorter applied works on prayer, divine service, and the study of Chassidus, several of which are the "founding documents" of Tomchei Temimim; (3) **Sichos / Talks** — informal festival-table talks, collected in *Toras Shalom*; and (4) **Letters (Igros Kodesh)** — his correspondence on communal, halachic, and spiritual matters. Unlike some predecessors he wrote relatively little freestanding halacha; his halachic and textual contributions take the form of **hagahos** (glosses) on classic Chabad texts. The original texts are overwhelmingly in **Hebrew** (Chassidic-philosophical register), with the *sichos* of *Toras Shalom* in **Yiddish**; English exists only for a selected subset.

A note on the digital originals: **chabadlibrary.org** is primarily the online catalog/book-reader of the Library of Agudas Chassidei Chabad, and its Rashab section digitizes only four works in full text — *Hemshech Ayin Beis*, *Kuntres U'Maayan*, *Toras Shalom*, and *Igros Kodesh*. The broader free full-text corpus (including the yearly *Sefer HaMaamarim* and *Samach Vov*) is more reliably found on **otzar770.com / chabadlibrarybooks.com** and as scans on **hebrewbooks.org**. Hosting is therefore noted per-work below rather than asserted uniformly.

## Complete Works

### Chassidus — Maamarim (Chassidic Discourses)

**ספר המאמרים — Sefer HaMaamarim** ("Book of Discourses," organized by year)
- *Type:* Chassidic discourses (maamarim). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* Close to 40 published volumes of maamarim; the year-by-year sets cover (Hebrew years) 5643/44, 5646–5651, 5652/53, 5654–5665, 5668–5671, 5672–5680, plus thematic *Likkutim* compilation volumes. *Period:* delivered across his leadership (1880s–1920); published progressively by Kehot from the mid-20th century onward under the seventh Rebbe's direction. *Description:* the core body of his formal teachings, each volume gathering the discourses he delivered in a given year.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew scans on **hebrewbooks.org** (search "ספר המאמרים תרס/תרע…") and on **otzar770.com / chabadlibrarybooks.com**; print volumes for purchase at **kehotonline.com** (e.g., *Sefer Hamaamarim 5680*, product HRR-SM80). *(The yearly sets are not in the chabadlibrary.org full-text reader.)*
- **English:** No comprehensive English translation of the year-sets; only scattered individual discourses translated (e.g., in the Chasidic Heritage Series and on chabad.org). Effectively **no full English translation** of *Sefer HaMaamarim* as a whole.

**ספר המאמרים — ליקוטים — Sefer HaMaamarim — Likkutim** ("Collected/Assorted Discourses")
- *Type:* Compilation volumes of the Rashab's collected and assorted maamarim. *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* A distinct **2-volume** Kehot set (gathering discourses not slotted into the yearly sets). *Period:* discourses across his leadership; published by Kehot. *Description:* the named *Likkutim* companion to the year-by-year *Sefer HaMaamarim*, often overlapping multiple years.
- **Original online:** Hebrew scans on **hebrewbooks.org** and **otzar770.com**.
- **English:** No full English translation.

**המשך ר"ה תרס"ו — Hemshech Yom Tov Shel Rosh Hashanah 5666** ("Samach Vov," סמ"ו)
- *Type:* Continuous multi-year discourse series (hemshech). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* ~61 installments/discourses spanning roughly two years (5666–5668 / 1905–1908); one large volume. *Period:* begun Rosh Hashanah 5666; first published 5731/1971–72 by Kehot under the seventh Rebbe; emended edition 2005. *Description:* a sweeping, encyclopedic exposition on the purpose of Creation and Torah's role; one of the single largest unified works of Chabad philosophy and a yeshiva cornerstone.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew scans on **hebrewbooks.org** and **otzar770.com**; print at **store.kehotonline.com** (verify product code on the live store).
- **English:** Partial only — Rabbi Yehuda (Hanan) Fenton's translation of the opening discourses (the first ~9 maamarim) is published online at **chayenu.org/5666**; audio classes are hosted at **theyeshiva.net** and **insidechassidus.org** (audio only, not the text). **No complete English translation.**

**המשך בשעה שהקדימו תער"ב — Hemshech BeSha'ah SheHikdimu 5672** ("Ayin Beis," ע"ב)
- *Type:* Continuous multi-year discourse series (hemshech). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* **144 discourses** by the commonly cited count (Wikipedia, Chabad.org); some sources give a lower figure (Chabadpedia ~124 actually delivered, since several maamarim were written but never delivered publicly — a hand-built index of the chabadlibrary digitization yields 141). *Period:* begun 1912 (5672), continuing several years; first complete edition published 5737/1976–77 by Kehot from manuscript, in **3 volumes**. A current Kehot **NEW edition** runs to **5 volumes** (set code HRR-SM72BESH.S), with ongoing per-booklet translations numbered into the 30s. *Description:* his most ambitious series — a systematic, accessible treatment of the deepest concepts of Chassidus; together with Samach Vov, the backbone of advanced Chabad study.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew full text on **chabadlibrary.org** (Rashab section, work id 3600000000) and scans on **hebrewbooks.org**; print — the 5-volume new edition at **store.kehotonline.com** (HRR-SM72BESH.S).
- **English:** Partial — a centennial (2012) selection was translated; Kehot issues ongoing booklet translations (maamarim grouped per booklet, numbered into the 30s — e.g. Booklet #32, Maamorim 117–120). **No complete English translation** of all volumes.

### Tracts — Kuntreisim (incl. Tomchei Temimim "founding documents")

**קונטרס ומעין מבית ה' — Kuntres U'Maayan miBeis Hashem** ("And a Spring from the House of G-d")
- *Type:* Applied Chassidic tract (sequence of discourses on Joel 4:18). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* one tract / short volume. *Period:* authored 1903 (5663). *Description:* a practical guide to identifying and uprooting one's negative character traits and base impulses — a manual of self-refinement.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew full text on **chabadlibrary.org** (Rashab section) and scans on **hebrewbooks.org**; print at kehotonline.com.
- **English:** Yes — published as ***Overcoming Folly*** (Kehot; trans. Zalman I. Posner); audio learning on chabad.org.

**קונטרס התפילה — Kuntres HaTefillah** ("Tract on Prayer")
- *Type:* Applied tract on the dynamics of Chassidic prayer/contemplation (avodah). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* one tract. *Period:* early 1900s; a foundational text for Tomchei Temimim. *Description:* explains how to prepare for and engage in meditative prayer.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew on **hebrewbooks.org** / **otzar770.com**; print at store.kehotonline.com.
- **English:** Yes — ***Tract on Prayer*** (Kehot, Chasidic Heritage Series; trans. Y. Eliezer Danzinger), Hebrew/English. Purchase at kehot.org (product ERR-TRAC, verified).

**קונטרס העבודה — Kuntres HaAvodah** ("Tract on Divine Service")
- *Type:* Applied tract on avodah (divine service). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* one tract. *Period:* authored for the Temimim (early 1900s). *Description:* guidance on the inner work of serving G-d, urging contemplative, lengthy prayer.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew on **hebrewbooks.org** / **otzar770.com**; Kehot print edition (hku-avod) is **Hebrew-only**.
- **English:** Yes — published as ***Love Like Fire and Water: A Guide to Jewish Meditation*** (Kuntres HaAvodah), trans. David Sterne — an independent edition (not Kehot). *(There is no edition titled "On the Service of the Heart"; that title appears to be spurious.)*

**קונטרס עץ החיים — Kuntres Eitz HaChaim** ("Tree of Life")
- *Type:* Tract on the importance of studying Chassidus and on the conduct (darchei haChassidus) of Tomchei Temimim students. *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* one tract (the Rashab affixed his signature — read by Chassidim as a "contract" for the Temimim). *Period:* authored for the yeshiva; reprinted 5706/1946 with additions by the sixth Rebbe. *Description:* the educational charter and ethos of Tomchei Temimim.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew on **hebrewbooks.org** / **otzar770.com**; print at kehotonline.com.
- **English:** Yes — ***The Tree of Life*** (Kuntres Eitz HaChayim), trans. Eliyahu Touger (Kehot); free full English text on **chabad.org** at article aid/144473. *(Note: the works "On the Teachings of Chassidus" / "On Learning Chassidus," chabad.org aid/147465, were authored by the SIXTH Rebbe, the Rayatz — they are not by the Rashab and are not the English of this tract.)*

**חנוך לנער — Chanoch LaNa'ar** ("Educate the Youth")
- *Type:* Ethical will / educational tract. *Language:* Hebrew. *Period:* his first ethical will. *Description:* guidance on the upbringing and spiritual education of youth.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew scans on **hebrewbooks.org** / **otzar770.com**; print at kehotonline.com.
- **English:** No standalone full English translation widely available (selections/summaries only).

**מאמר היחלצו תרנ"ט — Heichaltzu 5659** (Discourse/tract on Ahavas Yisroel)
- *Type:* Discourse later issued as a tract on love of fellow Jews and the dangers of baseless hatred/divisiveness. *Language:* Hebrew. *Period:* delivered Simchas Torah 5659 (autumn 1898). *Description:* a classic Chabad treatment of ahavas Yisroel and machlokes.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew on **hebrewbooks.org** / **otzar770.com**.
- **English:** Yes — published as ***Lessons in Kuntreis Heichaltzu 5659: A Treatise on Ahavas Yisrael*** (Kehot). *(Translator/year not independently confirmed; an earlier citation attributing it to Eliezer Danzinger, 1999, could not be verified.)*

**קונטרס בעניני כולל חב"ד — Kuntres B'Inyanei Kolel Chabad**
- *Type:* Kuntres on the affairs and charity of Kolel Chabad (the Chabad charity supporting Jews in the Land of Israel). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* one short tract. *Period:* during his leadership. *Description:* listed among the Rashab's published kuntreisim in standard Chabad bibliographies; concerns the conduct and support of Kolel Chabad.
- **Original online:** Hebrew scans on **hebrewbooks.org**.
- **English:** No standalone English translation.

*(Additional individual maamarim issued as kuntreisim and translated in the Chasidic Heritage Series exist beyond those named here; the year-by-year Sefer HaMaamarim remains the primary home for most of these.)*

### Talks — Sichos

**תורת שלום – ספר השיחות — Toras Shalom — Sefer HaSichos** ("Teaching of Peace / Book of Talks")
- *Type:* Informal Chassidic talks (sichos) delivered at festival farbrengens. *Language:* Yiddish (with Hebrew). *Extent:* one principal volume (~408 pp.). *Period:* talks delivered chiefly on Simchas Torah, Yud-Tes Kislev, and Purim; an early/foundational volume in Kehot's publication of the Rashab's writings. *Description:* deep concepts explained expansively, interwoven with Chabad stories, conduct, and table interaction — a more narrative counterpart to the formal maamarim.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew/Yiddish full text on **chabadlibrary.org** (Rashab section) and scans on **hebrewbooks.org**; print (Yiddish) at store.kehotonline.com (HRR-TORA.Y) and kehot.org (HRR-TORA).
- **English:** **No full English translation**; only excerpts/quotations appear in English secondary works and on chabad.org.

### Letters — Igros

**אגרות קודש – אדמו"ר הרש"ב — Igros Kodesh — Rebbe Rashab** ("Holy Letters")
- *Type:* Collected correspondence (communal, halachic, spiritual, and personal guidance). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* **6 volumes** (Vol. 6 adds 162 newly discovered letters, 5646–5678 / 1886–1918). *Period:* letters span the 1880s–1918; published by Kehot. *Description:* his letters on leadership, the Zionism controversy, yeshiva affairs, and avodah.
- **Original online:** Free Hebrew full text on **chabadlibrary.org** (Rashab section) and scans on **hebrewbooks.org**; print volumes at **store.kehotonline.com** (HRR-IK1 … HRR-IK6).
- **English:** **No full English translation** of the Rashab's Igros Kodesh; individual letters translated piecemeal on chabad.org. *(Note: Kehot's English "Igros Kodesh – The English Letters" project covers the SEVENTH Rebbe, not the Rashab.)*

### Other — Glosses, Polemics, and Institutional Texts

**הגהות — Hagahos** (Glosses/Annotations)
- *Type:* Marginal notes and clarifications on foundational Chabad texts. *Language:* Hebrew. *Scope:* glosses on the **Tanya**, the **Siddur (im Dach)**, **Torah Ohr**, **Likkutei Torah**, and notes on *Patach Eliyahu* (5658). *Description:* concise textual/conceptual clarifications, typically printed within or alongside modern editions of those works.
- **Original online:** Found within the digital Tanya/Torah Ohr/Likkutei Torah editions on **hebrewbooks.org** and **otzar770.com**.
- **English:** No standalone English edition.

**Anti-Zionism / Haskalah polemics — incl. contribution to אור לישרים (*Ohr LaYesharim*)**
- *Type:* Rabbinic polemical letters/essays. *Language:* Hebrew. *Period:* early 1900s. *Description:* his arguments against secular Zionism and Enlightenment reforms (the polemic also threads through Kuntres U'Maayan and his letters); *Ohr LaYesharim* is a 1900 collection of rabbinic opposition to Zionism to which he contributed.
- **Original online:** *Ohr LaYesharim* and related letters on **hebrewbooks.org**; relevant letters also in Igros Kodesh on chabadlibrary.org.
- **English:** No full English translation; discussed in English secondary literature.

**Tomchei Temimim founding documents**
- *Note:* The Rashab did not leave a single "charter" document; the founding/ethos of **Yeshivas Tomchei Temimim** (Lubavitch, 1897) and **Toras Emes** (Hebron, 1911) is expressed through the kuntreisim listed above — chiefly **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim** (the educational charter), **Kuntres HaTefillah**, and **Kuntres HaAvodah** — together with founding letters in his Igros Kodesh. See those entries for original and English availability.

## Scope & Caveats

- **"Complete" is aspirational.** This catalog covers the Rashab's *published* corpus as released by Kehot and digitized on otzar770.com / hebrewbooks.org / chabadlibrary.org. His full output (~2,000 discourses per Chabad.org, close to 40 volumes of maamarim plus letters) is large, and editing/publication of his manuscripts has continued for decades; some material remains in manuscript or appears only in *reshimos* (notes) and brief references.
- **Year-set volume counts are approximate.** "~40 volumes" and the listed year-ranges reflect the standard Kehot series; exact counts vary by edition (some years are double/triple volumes, and *Likkutim* compilation volumes overlap multiple years). Treat all volume numbers as **approx.** unless tied to a specific product. The full corpus including *Likkutim* and *Igros* is cited as high as ~50 volumes.
- **Discourse count.** Chabad.org states he delivered ~2,000 discourses over his ~38 years of leadership; some secondary Chabad sources give ~1,500. The higher, primary-source figure is used here.
- **Discourse counts within the hemshechim vary.** Ayin Beis is most commonly cited at **144** discourses, but counts differ (Chabadpedia ~124 actually delivered; a hand-built index of the chabadlibrary full text yields 141), partly because some maamarim were written but never delivered publicly. Samach Vov is cited at ~61.
- **English is the major gap.** Only a minority of the corpus exists in English: the kuntreisim (Chasidic Heritage Series and independent editions) are reasonably well-served, but **Sefer HaMaamarim**, **Samach Vov** (partial only — first 9 maamarim at chayenu.org/5666), **Ayin Beis** (partial only — ongoing Kehot booklets), **Toras Shalom**, and **Igros Kodesh** have **no complete English translation**. Beware confusing Kehot's English "Igros Kodesh" (seventh Rebbe) with the Rashab's letters, and "On the Teachings/Learning of Chassidus" (sixth Rebbe) with the Rashab's *Kuntres Eitz HaChaim* ("The Tree of Life").
- **Still being released.** Kehot continues to issue emended/critical editions (notably the 5-volume new edition of Ayin Beis) and booklet translations, so English coverage is incrementally expanding.
- **Digital hosting is per-work.** chabadlibrary.org's Rashab section only digitizes four works in full text (Ayin Beis, Kuntres U'Maayan, Toras Shalom, Igros Kodesh); the yearly Sefer HaMaamarim and Samach Vov are found on otzar770.com / chabadlibrarybooks.com and as hebrewbooks.org scans. Do not assume identical free hosting for every item.
- **Verification.** Biographical dates, the ~61-installment count for Samach Vov, the 144-discourse / (now 5-volume) figure for Ayin Beis, the 6-volume Igros Kodesh, and the kuntreisim English titles were cross-checked across Wikipedia, Chabad.org, Chabadpedia, Kehot, and a hand-built index of the chabadlibrary full text. Where a precise figure could not be doubly confirmed it is marked "approx." or noted inline.
