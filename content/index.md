# The Seven Lubavitcher Rebbes — Complete-Works Library

A **catalog and sourcing map** of everything written and taught by the seven Rebbes of
the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty (1745–1994). This folder does **not** hold the texts
themselves — the full corpus runs to hundreds of thousands of pages, most of it under
active copyright (Kehot Publication Society). Instead, each file lists a Rebbe's
*complete published works* and tells you exactly **where to obtain each one**, in the
original (Hebrew / Yiddish / Aramaic) and in English where a translation exists.

> **Verified edition.** The volume counts, dates, attributions, and source links in these
> files were compiled by parallel research and then **adversarially fact-checked against the
> live digital libraries** (ChabadLibrary, HebrewBooks, Sefaria, Chabad.org, Sichos in
> English, Kehot). Where a figure is inherently moving — series still being released by
> Kehot — it is marked "approx." and dated. ~**102 works/series** are catalogued in total.

## The seven Rebbes

| # | Common name / title | Full name | Life | Leadership | Relation to predecessor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Alter Rebbe** (Baal HaTanya) | R. Schneur Zalman of Liadi | 1745–1812 | c. 1788–1812 | Foremost disciple of the Maggid of Mezritch; founder of Chabad |
| 2 | **Mitteler Rebbe** | R. Dovber Schneuri | 1773–1827 | 1813–1827 | Son of the Alter Rebbe |
| 3 | **Tzemach Tzedek** | R. Menachem Mendel Schneersohn | 1789–1866 | c. 1828–1866 (formal 1831) | Grandson of the Alter Rebbe; nephew & son-in-law of the Mitteler Rebbe |
| 4 | **Rebbe Maharash** | R. Shmuel Schneersohn | 1834–1882 | 1866–1882 | Youngest son of the Tzemach Tzedek |
| 5 | **Rebbe Rashab** | R. Sholom Dovber Schneersohn | 1860–1920 | 1882–1920 (formal acceptance 1893) | Son of the Maharash |
| 6 | **Frierdiker Rebbe** (Rayatz) | R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn | 1880–1950 | 1920–1950 | Only son of the Rashab |
| 7 | **The Rebbe** | R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson | 1902–1994 | 1951–1994 | Son-in-law of the Rayatz; descendant of the Tzemach Tzedek |

## Sourcing legend

Short names used in the catalogs (full details in
[digital-sources-guide.md](digital-sources-guide.md)):

- **ChabadLibrary** — [chabadlibrary.org](https://chabadlibrary.org), Chabad's official
  free digital library (*Otzar Sifrei Lubavitch*); nearly the entire Hebrew corpus online.
- **HebrewBooks** — [hebrewbooks.org](https://hebrewbooks.org); free PDF scans, including
  most older Chabad printings.
- **Sefaria** — [sefaria.org](https://www.sefaria.org); free, open API; growing Chassidut
  library, many texts bilingual.
- **Chabad.org** — [chabad.org/library](https://www.chabad.org/library); the main English
  gateway — *Lessons in Tanya*, *Hayom Yom*, selected *Likkutei Sichos* and *Igros Kodesh*.
- **SIE** — Sichos in English / Kehot, [sie.org](https://www.sie.org); the largest body of
  English translations, many as free PDFs.
- **Kehot** — [kehot.com](https://www.kehot.com) / store.kehotonline.com, the official
  publisher; authoritative print/eBook editions.
