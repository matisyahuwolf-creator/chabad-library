# Bibliography

Every source used in this section, with how far it was checked.

- **VERIFIED**: the abstract, full text or page was read for the specific claim used.
- **PARTLY**: the citation is confirmed; some content comes from a secondary source.
- **UNVERIFIED**: could not be confirmed; not relied on.

Science sources were checked on 8 October 2026 through Europe PMC, PMC full text, Crossref,
publisher pages, SEC filings and the Federal Register. Hebrew sources were checked against the
Chabad corpus search used for this library.
