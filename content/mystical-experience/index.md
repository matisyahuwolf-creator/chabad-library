# Mystical experience: a research framework

**The question.** Since 2006, psychedelic science has measured mystical experience with one
main instrument, the **Mystical Experience Questionnaire (MEQ30)**, and has reported that
people who score high on it tend to change more afterwards. Chabad Chassidus has been studying
the same kind of moment, by contemplation rather than by drug, since the 1790s. What does each
side know that the other does not?

**The short answer.** The science measures *how much* was felt. Chabad asks *whose* arousal it
was, whether the self was felt inside it, whether it returned to life, and what remained
afterwards. Its sources say the deepest arousal is the least self-felt, that intensity can be a
sign of distance, that ecstasy which "wants to leave the body" leaves "no trace," and that the
remedy for an experience too big to hold is a bigger vessel, not a smaller light. Each of those
can be turned into a measurement, and none has been tested.

## How claims are labelled

- **Stated**: a source says it. Science sources were checked against the abstract or full text
  where possible; Hebrew was returned by or checked against the Chabad corpus search used for
  this library.
- **Ours**: our mapping or interpretation. Argue with it.
- **Untested**: something measurable that nobody has measured.
- **OCR**: a Hebrew passage read from a scanned page; the reference is reliable, the exact
  wording is not.

Where a fact could not be confirmed, the page says so instead of guessing.

---

## Scope and cautions

- This is a framework for **research**, not guidance for practice. Nothing here is advice to
  take a psychedelic, and nothing here should be read as a halachic ruling.
- The comparison is between a *questionnaire tradition* and a *textual tradition*. The Chabad
  sources were not written with the MEQ in mind; every mapping between them is ours.
- Related material in the Only One research shelf: *The psychological parallel, part B*
  (adverse effects and counterfeits) and *Guiding a modern person into the inner world* both
  touch the MEQ30, Hood's scale and the Ego Dissolution Inventory from the other direction.
