# Translation glossary: modern words ↔ Chassidus

People describe inner experience in the language of psychedelics, meditation, therapy and
psychology. Chassidus has its own, more exact vocabulary for the same territory. This glossary
lets a guide (human or AI) **meet a person in their words, find the Chabad term, say where the
two agree, say where they differ, and know what to ask next.**

Each entry has five parts:

- **They say**: the phrases people actually use.
- **Chassidus**: the nearest term.
- **Same**: what the two share.
- **Not the same**: the distinction. This is the part that matters most.
- **Guide**: what to ask or do.

The entries draw on the sources verified on [page 4](4-what-chassidus-adds.md) and
[page 3](3-beyond-the-drug.md). Where an entry is our own mapping, it says **Ours**. Hebrew is
quoted only where the corpus check confirmed it.

## A. Self and its dissolving

### 1. Ego dissolution · ego death · "I disappeared" · "no self"
- **Chassidus:** *bittul* (self-nullification), in two grades: *bittul hayesh* (the self stops
  claiming importance) and *bittul bimetzius* (the self does not register as a separate thing).
- **Same:** the ordinary self-centre goes quiet.
- **Not the same:** ego dissolution is something that *happens to* you, and it is often felt as a
  dramatic event. In Chassidus, a *bittul* that is strongly felt is the lower kind. In the world of
  Tohu, "the nullification was felt" («הביטול הוא במורגש»), and Tohu broke (Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674,
  §601). True *bittul* leaves a person present, able and serving, not erased.
- **Guide:** "While it was happening, were you aware of *yourself* dissolving, or was there no
  'you' watching?" Then: "What are you doing differently since?"

### 2. Loss of boundaries · merging · "I became everything"
- **Chassidus:** *ratzo* (running out), *klos hanefesh* (the soul's expiry; yearning to be
  absorbed).
- **Same:** a real pull beyond limits.
- **Not the same:** the Rebbe calls ratzo without return "the plague in spiritual service": "the
  yearning to leave all measure and limit... in total nullification" (Likkutei Sichos vol. 37,
  Tazria). The Rebbe Rashab's index: "the running to be absorbed is also existence" (Sefer
  HaMaamarim 5666, index; OCR).
- **Guide:** honour the pull, then ask where it is taking them: *back* into life, or *away* from it.
- **Safety:** "wanting to leave my body", "not wanting to come back" may be spiritual longing or
  may be danger. The guide's safety rule comes first; ask the one deciding question.

### 3. Humility · "I felt small" · self-diminishment (awe research)
- **Chassidus:** *shiflus* (lowliness), *anivus* (humility).
- **Not the same:** the Rebbe Rashab separates the humble person, who "exists and is in total
  bittul", from the lowly one who "finds no worth or existence in himself at all"; Moshe, the
  humble one, "was a vessel for the Essence" (Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674, §613:1). Feeling worthless
  is not the goal.
- **Guide:** watch for smallness turning into self-erasure or shame.

## B. Unity

### 4. "All is One" · nonduality · oneness · "everything is connected"
- **Chassidus:** *yichuda ila'ah* (higher unity: "all before Him is as nothing") and *yichuda
  tata'ah* (lower unity: the world exists and is nullified to its source) (Likkutei Torah, Re'eh
  4:15).
- **Same:** the many are seen as one.
- **Not the same:** in Chabad these are two vantage points reached by two *contemplations*, not
  two sensations, and the goal is to join them: "the higher unity drawn into the lower" (Toras
  Menachem vol. 45). Oneness that makes the world unreal is half.
- **Guide:** "Did the world feel *unreal*, or *more* real and alive?" Lead toward the second.

### 5. "Everything was alive" · "I saw the energy in things" · living presence
- **Chassidus:** the *chayus* (divine life-force) in every creature, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 3.
- **Not the same:** the Tanya says *if* the eye could see the life-force, the physical "would not be
  seen by our eyes at all." Normally it is **not** seen; it is known by understanding. A vision is
  not required, and a vision is not proof.
- **Guide:** offer the contemplation, not the expectation of seeing.

### 6. Pure consciousness · "awareness without content" · emptiness
- **Chassidus:** *ayin* (nothingness); *bittul bimetzius*; above that, *Atzmus* (the Essence),
  which is neither something nor nothing.
- **Ours:** close to Stace's "introvertive unity" and the MEQ's internal unity.
- **Not the same:** for Chabad the Essence is found in the *world* as much as beyond it (*dirah
  betachtonim*, a dwelling below). Emptiness is not the destination.

## C. Feeling and intensity

### 7. Bliss · ecstasy · euphoria · "best feeling of my life" · peak experience
- **Chassidus:** *hispa'alus* (arousal), *ta'anug* (delight), *ahavah b'taanugim* (love of delights).
- **Same:** Chassidus wants the heart moved; it is not cold.
- **Not the same:** intensity is not the measure. "An arousal he does not feel at all: this arousal
  is the deepest" («התפעלות שאינו מרגיש כלל וכלל שהתפעלות זו עמוקה ביותר», Pirush HaMilos). The
  felt vessel of *nogah* "is felt in great addition... and this addition is a lack" (Kuntres
  HaHitpa'alut 9:5). Heat can be a sign of distance (Sefer HaMaamarim 5666; OCR).
- **Guide:** never praise intensity. Ask: "Was it about *what you saw*, or about *how good it felt
  to you*?" (Ayin Beis: arousal at "the good of the thing itself" vs "how it is good for me").

### 8. Awe · wonder · "I was blown away"
- **Chassidus:** *yirah* (awe/fear), *bittul* before greatness.
- **Not the same:** the Rebbe Rashab's index of Samach Vav: "from a wondrous thing grasped in
  comprehension: tremendous arousal, but no nullification of the faculties" (OCR). Being amazed and
  being changed are different.
- **Guide:** "What did the wonder ask of you?"

### 9. Emotional breakthrough · release · crying it out · catharsis
- **Chassidus:** *lev nishbar* (a broken heart), *merirus* (bitterness, constructive regret),
  *teshuvah*.
- **Not the same:** the Rebbe Rashab: release "without an accounting... is only arousal, without an
  object and without benefit"; with inner feeling tied to particulars, "he is transformed in his
  very essence" (Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674, §613:2).
- **Guide:** help them name the *specifics* the release was about.

### 10. Numb · flat · "I felt nothing" · dry practice
- **Chassidus:** *timtum halev* (a dulled heart); words "from the lips outward" (Kuntres
  HaHitpa'alut 1:17–18).
- **Not the same:** Chassidus says the soul's essence "is to be stirred always" even when the
  faculties are dull (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 1:19), so despair is ruled out. And a quiet, unfelt
  state may be the *deep* one (entry 7).
- **Safety:** numbness may be depression. Never read it as a spiritual failing.
- **Guide:** "Nothing felt" is not "nothing happened". Ask what they did afterward.

## D. Knowing

### 11. Insight · "I just *knew*" · download · revelation · noetic quality
- **Chassidus:** *da'as*: "binding... he binds his mind with a very strong and firm bond, fixes his
  thought... and does not let his mind turn away" (Tanya 3:13). *Hasagah* (comprehension).
- **Same:** real knowing has a felt certainty.
- **Not the same:** certainty that comes from a feeling is the counterfeit's signature. The Mitteler
  Rebbe's test is growth: the real thing keeps opening ("to understand one thing from another"); the
  shallow thing "keeps shrinking... until only the outline remains" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 21:11–14).
  Science agrees on the risk: psychedelics raise "the subjective intensity of insights... including
  false ones" (McGovern et al. 2024).
- **Guide:** "Can you still unfold it, or has it become a sentence you repeat?"

### 12. "It was more real than reality" · encountering ultimate reality
- **Chassidus:** *emes* (truth); *giluy* (revelation).
- **Not the same:** the Mitteler Rebbe names a counterfeit "from a confusion of the brain's vessels,
  or from an overheating of the blood", in which a person "mixes falsehood and truth together"
  (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 21:8). How real it felt does not settle what it was; what it produced does.

### 13. Visions · lights · seeing God · felt presence
- **Chassidus:** *dimyon* (imagination), which can serve or mislead.
- **Not the same:** Kuntres HaTefillah's contents warn that "external arousal is false imagination"
  and that the inexperienced are "liable to err" (OCR). Research: felt presence is predicted by
  absorption and suggestion (Granqvist et al. 2005).
- **Guide:** neither confirm nor dismiss; ask what it moved them to do.

## E. Time, words, holiness

### 14. Timelessness · "time stopped" · eternal now
- **Chassidus:** *lema'alah min hazman* (above time).
- **Ours:** in Chabad this is a teaching about G-d, to be understood. Losing track of time is not a
  sign of having reached it.

### 15. "No words for it" · ineffable · beyond language
- **Chassidus:** the *niggun* (wordless melody); the "cry of the heart" beyond letters (Samach Vav;
  OCR).
- **Ours:** ineffability is a rung, not a proof. The next step in Chassidus is to bring it back into
  words, study and deeds.
- **Guide:** "Try anyway. What's the first word?"

### 16. Sacred · holy · "church feeling" · reverence
- **Chassidus:** *kedushah*. G-d "rests only on what is nullified to Him"
  («אֵין הַקָּדוֹשׁ־בָּרוּךְ־הוּא שׁוֹרֶה אֶלָּא עַל דָּבָר שֶׁבָּטֵל אֶצְלוֹ», Tanya 6:5).
- **Not the same:** holiness tracks how little the self is in the way, not how strongly "holy" is
  felt.

## F. Afterward

### 17. Integration · "bringing it back" · comedown · afterglow
- **Chassidus:** *shov* (return); *dirah betachtonim* (making a dwelling for G-d below).
- **Same:** the experience must come into life.
- **Not the same:** in Chassidus the return is not cleanup after the real thing; it is the real
  thing. "Running without returning is death" (Hayom Yom, 27 Adar II). And "when the running is
  truly nullification, the return is also nullification: as in keeping Torah and mitzvos" (Ayin
  Beis, Re'eh 5674, §601:2).
- **Guide:** every conversation about a high ends with one concrete thing to do.

### 18. "It faded" · "I lost it" · chasing the high · needing another trip/retreat
- **Chassidus:** natural love and fear "fall, and return, and must be stirred again"; the sign of the
  real kind is "constancy" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 14:12). Arousal "for the moment" that wants "to leave
  the body... no trace of it remains" (Ayin Beis §613:3).
- **Ours (the Tohu model):** a light bigger than its vessel does not settle. The answer is a
  bigger vessel (study, character, practice), not a bigger light. "To draw the abundant lights of
  Tohu into the broad vessels of Tikkun" (Toras Menachem vol. 34).
- **Guide:** shift from "how do I get it back?" to "what vessel can I build to hold it?"

### 19. Set and setting · intention · container · trip sitter
- **Chassidus:** *hachanah* (preparation before prayer, Kuntres HaTefillah); the *mashpia*
  (mentor); the *farbrengen*. The Alter Rebbe, as the Rebbe often quoted: "what a Chassidic
  farbrengen can accomplish, even the angel Michael cannot" (Igros Kodesh vol. 16).
- **Same:** context shapes the experience; research agrees (communitas, Kettner et al. 2021).

### 20. Bad trip · challenging experience · spiritual emergency · meditation gone wrong
- **Chassidus:** the counterfeit arousal; *shevirah* (shattering); a heart "clogged" at the hour of
  opportunity.
- **Not the same, and important:** distress, fear, panic, dissociation may be medical or
  psychological. **Safety first.** Never read illness as a spiritual state.
- **Research:** 31% significant fear at high psilocybin doses (Griffiths 2006); 6–14% lasting bad
  effects in mindfulness programs (Britton 2021).

## G. Substances and practice

### 21. "Psychedelics are a shortcut to God" · microdosing for spirituality · plant medicine
- **Chassidus:** the Rebbe, 1964: drug use "is not the proper way to attain mystical inspiration";
  "The Jewish way is to go from strength to strength"; drugs "have a place only if they are necessary
  for the physical health"; "first utilize all one's natural capacities."
- **Also on record:** the Rebbe Rayatz on *mashke* (spirits) at a farbrengen: it may need scrubbing
  "with iron brushes" the next day, yet "today one must take mashke."
- **Guide:** do not shame a person for a past experience. Meet what they saw; offer the natural path;
  medical decisions and halachic questions go to their doctor and their rav.

### 22. Meditation · mindfulness · "clearing the mind" · breathwork
- **Chassidus:** *hisbonenus*: detailed contemplation of a G-dly idea, building understanding until
  the heart is moved by itself.
- **Not the same:** most meditation research studies practices that *loosen* thought
  (deconstructive). Hisbonenus *builds* thought (constructive) (Dahl, Lutz & Davidson 2015 typology;
  **Ours** to place it there). Emptying the mind is not the method; filling it with a true idea is.
- **Also:** the Rebbe in 1978–79 pressed doctors to build a *kosher* therapeutic meditation for
  anyone, without religious preconditions.

### 23. Flow · "in the zone" · absorption
- **Chassidus:** *dveikus* (cleaving) in action; the beinoni whose "mind rules the heart" (Tanya 12:5).
- **Not the same:** the Tanya's measure is conduct, not states: "not all times are alike" in the
  heart, but deed, speech and thought are in a person's power (Tanya 14:1).
- **Research:** some people have a trait talent for absorption (Tellegen); Tanya: "each according to
  his measure" (Tanya 50:5). No shame in being less moved.

## Using this in a chatbot

1. **Match by their words, reply in theirs first.** The "They say" line is the index. Name the
   Chabad term only after reflecting their experience.
2. **Lead with the distinction**, gently, as a question rather than a correction.
3. **End toward return**: a concrete step back into life (entry 17).
4. **Safety overrides every entry** (entries 2, 10, 20).
5. **Quote only verified Hebrew**; the references here are the checked ones.
