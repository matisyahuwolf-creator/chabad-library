# What Chabad Chassidus adds to the mystical-experience story

The psychedelic literature measures **how much** of a mystical experience a person had, mostly
with the MEQ30 ([page 1](1-the-meq.md)), and asks whether that amount predicts later change
([page 2](2-psychedelic-research.md)). Chabad's contemplative literature, written from 1786 to
1992, asks different questions of the same kind of moment: **where did it come from, whose
arousal was it, did the person feel themselves in it, and what is left afterwards?** It also
has a word for an experience that is intense and does not last.

This page sets the two side by side. Every Hebrew line below was returned by, or checked
against, the Chassidus corpus search used for this library; the reference is given. Where a
source was read from a scanned page (OCR), that is said, and its wording is approximate.

**How to read the labels.**
**Stated** = the source says it. **Ours** = our mapping or interpretation; argue with it.
**Untested** = a claim that could be measured and has not been.

---

## 1. The map: MEQ30 factors against Chabad categories

| MEQ30 factor (Barrett, Johnson & Griffiths 2015) | Nearest Chabad category | Where Chassidus agrees | Where it parts ways |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mystical: internal unity** (pure awareness, loss of the usual self) | *Bittul bimetzius* (nullification of existence); *yichuda ila'ah* (the higher unity, "all is as nothing before Him") | A state in which the separate self does not register is real and high | The *wish* to be absorbed is itself a form of self (§5). Dissolution is not the goal; a self that serves is |
| **Mystical: external unity** ("all is One") | *Yichuda tata'ah* (the lower unity); Shaar HaYichud ch. 3, "the vitality and spirituality in every creature" | The world can be known as nullified to the divine life in it | It is reached by understanding, not by perception, and the two unities must be joined (§3) |
| **Mystical: noetic quality** (certainty of contact with ultimate reality) | *Da'as*: binding the mind and holding it | Real contemplative knowing has a felt certainty | Certainty from a feeling is the counterfeit's signature; *da'as* is a held understanding, tested by what grows out of it (§6) |
| **Mystical: sacredness** (awe, holiness) | *Kedushah* defined as *bittul*: what is nullified to G-d is holy (Tanya ch. 6) | Holiness is a real quality of the moment | Holiness is measured by how little the self is felt, not by how strongly the holy is felt (§2) |
| **Positive mood** (joy, peace, ecstasy) | *Hispa'alus* (arousal), *simcha*, *ahavah b'taanugim* (love of delights) | Chassidus wants arousal; it is not a cold discipline | Felt intensity is ranked *low*; heat can be a sign of distance (§2) |
| **Transcendence of time and space** | *Lema'alah min hazman* (above time) | The Essence is above time and place | This is a teaching to be understood, not a perceptual marker of depth |
| **Ineffability** | The *niggun* (wordless melody); the "cry of the heart" beyond letters | What touches the point of the soul cannot be put into letters | Ineffability is a rung, not proof; the next rung is to bring it back into words and deeds |

**Ours.** The mapping is ours; no Chabad text was written with Stace or the MEQ in view. It is
offered as a working hypothesis for the studies proposed on [page 5](5-research-program.md).

## 2. The intensity paradox: the deeper arousal is the one you do not feel

The MEQ30 is a sum of intensity ratings: the more strongly each feature was felt, the higher
the score. The Mitteler Rebbe's *Kuntres HaHitpa'alut* (Tract on Ecstasy, 1813) builds a
whole diagnostic on the opposite axis: **how much the self was felt inside the experience.**

**Stated.**

> שבקדושה בחינת הכלי שהוא בחינת הישות של האור הוא בבחינת הבטול בתכלית, ובחינת הכלי דנוג״ה יש מורגש בתוספות מרובה על העיקר ותוספות זה גרעון

*In holiness, the vessel (the "somethingness" in which the light is held) is in total
nullification; the vessel of nogah is felt, in great addition beyond the essence, and this
addition is a lack.* (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 9:5)

> ומה שיוגרע יותר ולא ירגיש הרי זה בא מצד תוספת האור וחיות פנימית ביותר

*And the more it is diminished and not felt, this comes from an increase of light and of
inward vitality.* (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 9:12)

He calls the loud, visible kind "the great exaggeration seen in external arousal"
(«הגוזמא רבה הנראית בהתפעלות חיצונית», 9:9). In another work he states it flatly:

> התפעלות שאינו מרגיש כלל וכלל שהתפעלות זו עמוקה ביותר

*An arousal he does not feel at all: this arousal is the deepest.*
(Pirush HaMilos, "Ivdu es Hashem b'simcha", 98b)

He does not say such a state is unfelt in every sense. Of the *niggun*, the wordless melody,
he says it is sudden and unwilled, "as if he does not feel or know it at all at that very
moment; this is called absence of self-sensation, and nevertheless it is a felt arousal"
(«ומפני שזהו כאלו אינו מרגיש ונודע לו כלל באותה שעה ממש זהו הנקרא העדר הרגשת עצמו בזה כלל, אבל מכל מקום התפעלות מורגשת הוא», 9:22).
The distinction is between **feeling the experience** and **feeling oneself having it.**

The Rebbe Rashab adds a physical image. Heat, he says, belongs to distance:

> כמו המשתוקק לדבר שמרוחק ממנו, שהוא בהתפעלות וחמימות עצומה ... דכאשר הוא בבחי' ריחוק מאלקות ... ה"ה בבחי' התפעלות

*Like one who longs for a thing far from him, who is in tremendous arousal and heat... when
one is distant from G-dliness, one is in arousal.* (Sefer HaMaamarim 5666, scan p. 179;
**OCR**, wording approximate)

And the Mitteler Rebbe notes that the peak itself is a narrowing: at the moment the heart is
moved, "the whole extended length of the divine matter that moved him contracts, and nothing
remains of it but what touches the heart's arousal" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 14:2).

**What this adds.** The MEQ30 cannot separate two people who both rate "experience of
amazement" a 5: one who was flooded with self-feeling, and one in whom the self barely
registered. Chassidus says these are opposite states. **Untested:** whether self-salience
*during* the peak (not after) predicts persisting change, independent of MEQ score.

## 3. Two unities, and the instruction to join them

Stace, whose categories the MEQ is built on, split mystical unity into *introvertive* (unity
without content) and *extrovertive* (unity seen in the many). Chabad has its own pair.

**Stated.** The Alter Rebbe:

> ב׳ דעות יחודא עילאה ויחודא תתאה יחודא עילאה מלמעלה למטה דכולא קמיה כל׳ כו׳. ויחודא תתאה ממטה למעלה בחי׳ יש מאין

*Two kinds of knowledge: the higher unity and the lower unity. The higher unity is from above
to below, "all before Him is as nothing"; the lower unity is from below to above, the aspect
of something-from-nothing.* (Likkutei Torah, Re'eh 4:15)

The extrovertive side is described in the Tanya in terms close to Stace's "sense of life or
living presence in all things" and the MEQ30's "all is One":

> אילו ניתנה רשות לעין לראות ולהשיג את החיות ורוחניות שבכל נברא ... לא היה גשמיות הנברא וחומרו וממשו נראה כלל לעינינו כי הוא בטל במציאות ממש

*If the eye were permitted to see and grasp the vitality and spirituality in every creature...
the creature's physicality, matter and substance would not be seen by our eyes at all, for it
is truly nullified in existence.* (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 3:2)

Note the "if": in the Tanya this is normally **not** seen. It is known by contemplation.

The index of the Rebbe Rashab's *Hemshech 5666* sorts the two nullifications by the two
contemplations that produce them: contemplating G-dliness enclothed in the worlds yields
*bittul hayesh*; contemplating G-dliness above the worlds yields *bittul bimetzius*
(Sefer HaMaamarim 5666–5667, index s.v. ביטול, scan p. 375; **OCR**, and an editor's index
summary, not the Rashab's own sentence).

The Lubavitcher Rebbe insists neither is enough alone:

> שצריך להיות הן יחודא תתאה והן יחודא עילאה, והיינו, שלא מספיק אחד מהם, אלא צריכים להיות שניהם דוקא, ובאופן שגם ביחודא תתאה צריך להיות נמשך יחודא עילאה

*There must be both the lower unity and the higher unity: one of them is not enough; both
are needed, and in such a way that the higher unity is drawn into the lower.*
(Toras Menachem vol. 45 [5726, part 1], Shabbos Mikeitz–Chanukah, §90, citing Kuntres Eitz HaChaim)

**What this adds.** The MEQ30 sums internal and external unity into one "mystical" factor.
Chabad treats them as two different achievements reached by two different contemplations,
and ranks a third thing above both: the higher unity *held inside* ordinary life.
**Untested:** whether internal and external unity items behave differently as predictors.
**Ours:** the alignment of Stace's pair with the Chabad pair is approximate. Stace, too,
reports that mystics take the One of both experiences to be the same One. The difference is in
what each does with the pair: Stace classifies two *types of experience*; Chabad describes two
*vantage points* reached by two *contemplations*, and ranks their joining above either.

## 4. Tohu and Tikkun: a native theory of the experience that shatters

Kabbalah, as Chabad teaches it, begins creation with a world of *Tohu* (chaos): "many lights
and few vessels," which broke. Then comes *Tikkun* (repair): many vessels, fewer lights.

**Stated.** The Rebbe Rashab describes Tohu's inner life in words close to a description of
intense unitive experience:

> רק הי' בהם ההתכללות במקורם בבחי' מורגש ביותר ... הי' בהם בחי' הרצוא להכלל במקורם כו', רק שלא הי' בבחי' הנחה וביטול עצמם כ"א בבחי' מורגש ... ובתהו הי' המורגש ביותר

*They had absorption into their source, in a mode felt very strongly... they had the yearning
to be absorbed into their source, but not as a setting-aside and nullification of themselves:
as something felt... and in Tohu, the felt was at its greatest.*
(Hemshech Ayin Beis, part 1, Re'eh 5674, §601:1)

The Rebbe states the purpose of the whole work as bringing that intensity into vessels that
can hold it:

> שזהו תכלית ענין העבודה לחבר ולהמשיך את האורות מרובים דתהו בכלים רחבים דתיקון

*This is the purpose of the service: to join and draw the abundant lights of Tohu into the
broad vessels of Tikkun.* (Toras Menachem vol. 34 [5722, part 3], Shabbos Shelach, §7)

**Ours.** This is the strongest conceptual contribution. Psychedelic research knows that
large acute experiences sometimes fail to last, and sometimes destabilise (challenging
experiences, persisting distress; see [page 2](2-psychedelic-research.md)). It has no theory
of *why* except dose, set, setting and "integration." Chabad has one: a light larger than its
vessel is felt intensely, does not settle, and breaks; the remedy is not a smaller light but a
**larger vessel**, built by understanding, character work and practice. In this reading a
high-dose psilocybin session is a Tohu event, and integration is Tikkun.
**Untested:** whether pre-existing "vessel" measures (contemplative training, psychological
flexibility, depth of a person's framework for the experience) moderate the link between MEQ
score and lasting benefit.

## 5. Ratzo v'shov: dissolution without return is called a sickness

The MEQ treats loss of self and boundaries as the core of the experience. Chabad calls the
*yearning* for that loss *ratzo* (running out) and says it is incomplete, and even dangerous,
without *shov* (return).

**Stated.** The Lubavitcher Rebbe:

> שענין ה"נגע" בעבודה רוחנית הוא "רצוא" בלי "שוב", היינו העבודה ד"כלות הנפש", שהיא התשוקה לצאת ממדידות והגבלות ולידבק באלקות בביטול גמור, ואין זה כפי הכוונה העליונה, שצ"ל העבודה ד"שוב" דוקא, להמשיך אלקות בעולם, ע"י העבודה דתורה ומצוות, נשמה בגוף דוקא

*The "plague" in spiritual service is "running" without "returning": the service of the
soul's expiry, the yearning to leave all measure and limit and cleave to G-dliness in total
nullification. This is not the supernal intent, which requires the service of "returning"
specifically: drawing G-dliness into the world through Torah and mitzvos, a soul in a body
specifically.* (Likkutei Sichos vol. 37, Tazria)

> ורצוא בלי שוב, היא מיתה

*And running without returning is death.* (Hayom Yom, 27 Adar II, "from my father's talk",
i.e. the Rebbe Rashab as recorded by the Rebbe Rayatz, on Nadav and Avihu)

The Rebbe Rashab goes further: the wish to be absorbed is itself a kind of self.
"The running to be absorbed is also existence (*metzius*), but the return that comes from
sensing the Essence is nullification of existence" (Sefer HaMaamarim 5666–5667, index s.v.
ביטול, entry סב; **OCR**, editor's summary). And he gives the test of a true ascent:

> כאשר הרצוא הוא בבחי' ביטול באמת גם השוב הוא בבחי' ביטול, כמו בקיום התומ"צ לקיים רצה"ע

*When the running is truly nullification, the return is also nullification: as in keeping
Torah and mitzvos to fulfil the Supreme Will.* (Hemshech Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674, §601:2)

**What this adds.** A descriptive questionnaire cannot tell an ascent that returns from one
that does not. Chassidus says the *return* is where the authenticity of the ascent shows.
**Untested:** a "return" measure (does the experience direct the person *toward* their body,
family, obligations and community, or away from them?) as a predictor of benefit and of harm.

## 6. Persistence: "no trace remains"

Psychedelic trials measure lasting change with follow-ups and the Persisting Effects
Questionnaire. Chabad sources give criteria, in the first person, for telling a moment that
will last from one that will not.

**Stated.** The Rebbe Rashab on a powerful arousal toward return (*teshuvah*):

> וכאשר הוא בלא חשבון ה"ז רק התפעלות בלי מבוקש ובלי תועלת

*When it is without an accounting, it is only arousal, without an object and without
benefit.*

> ובזה הוא מתהפך בעצם מהותו, אבל כאשר הוא בהתפעלות לבד, ה"ז בהתגברות ביותר לפי שעה, והוא שרוצה לצאת מהגוף לגמרי ועכ"פ להשליך כל עניני חייו מנגד, וזה רק מדמה לבד ומסתפק בזה, ואינו נשאר מזה שום רושם

*By this [inner feeling tied to particulars] he is transformed in his very essence. But when
it is arousal alone, it is very strong for the moment: he wants to leave the body entirely,
or at least to throw all his life's concerns aside. This is only imagining, and he is
satisfied with it, and no trace of it remains.*
(Hemshech Ayin Beis, part 1, Re'eh 5674, §613:2–3)

The Mitteler Rebbe gives two signs. Constancy:

> וסימן לזה הוא תמידות ההתפעלות בכוונה זאת ולא תפסוק כלל כמו דחילו ורחימו טבעיים שנופלים וחוזרים ומעוררים

*The sign of it is the constancy of the arousal in this intent; it does not stop at all,
unlike natural fear and love, which fall, and return, and must be stirred again.*
(Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 14:12)

And growth. The real thing keeps opening ("to understand one thing from another in tremendous
expansion, many times beyond the words he heard or saw written"), while what was only
grasped intellectually "keeps shrinking and grows distant from him over time, until it
shrinks entirely, and only the outline remains" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 21:11–14).

**What this adds.** Four candidate predictors, each measurable:
1. **Specificity:** was the experience tied to particulars of one's life ("an accounting"), or global?
2. **Direction:** did it pull toward leaving the body and casting life aside, or toward re-ordering it?
3. **Constancy:** in the weeks after, does it fall and need re-kindling, or does it hold?
4. **Generativity:** does understanding keep expanding, or does the memory shrink to an outline?

**Untested,** all four.

## 7. The counterfeit has a physical cause

**Stated.** The Mitteler Rebbe, writing in 1813, describes an arousal that mimics the divine
one and traces it to the body:

> והוא רק דמיון שוא מכח המדמה שבבינה שבנפשו שלמטה הרבה מן ההשגה שמזה באה סיבת כח הכוזב המדומה מחמת בלבול כלי המוח או מהתגברות רתיחות הדמים עד שמטעה את עצמו ביותר ושם אור לחושך וחושך לאור ומהפך ומבלבל ומערב שקר ואמת יחד

*It is only false imagination, from the imaginative power in his soul's understanding, far
below comprehension. From this comes the lying, imagined power: from a confusion of the
brain's vessels, or from an overheating of the blood, until he deceives himself badly, takes
light for darkness and darkness for light, inverts, confuses, and mixes falsehood and truth
together.* (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 21:7–8)

> והוא הטעות הנמצא ביותר באנשי שלומינו

*And this is the error found most among our own people.* (21:9)

**Ours.** A change in "the brain's vessels" is close to what a psychedelic is. The text does
**not** say that every experience with a physical cause is false; it says that this
counterfeit has a physical cause, and that it can be told apart by its fruit (§6). That is a
testable claim, and it lines up with the modern worry about "false insights" under
psychedelics ([page 2](2-psychedelic-research.md), critiques). The Rebbe Rashab's
*Kuntres HaTefillah* (1900) carries the same warning; its table of contents lists
"external arousal is false imagination" and "young men who have not studied much Chassidus
are liable to err" in imaginary arousal (Kuntres HaTefillah, contents, scan p. 34; **OCR**).

What the Mitteler Rebbe asks for instead:

> שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר

*That their soul's arousal be the arousal of G-dliness itself, and not the arousal of the
life of the flesh.* (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:3)

## 8. On substances: the Chabad record is not abstinence

Chassidim drink *mashke* (spirits) at a *farbrengen*, a gathering for mutual encouragement.
The sources treat it as a real opener and a real risk.

**Stated.** The Rebbe Rayatz, Purim 1927:

> עס קען זיין אַז די היינטיקע משקה וועט מען דאַרפן מאָרגן רייבן מיט אייזערנע בארשטן, וואָרום עס קען זיין דערפון אַ התפעלות המדות, התנשאות, גסות ... דאָס (המשקה) איז דאָך גשמיות ... אָבער היינט דאַרף מען נעמען משקה

*It may be that today's mashke will have to be scrubbed off tomorrow with iron brushes,
because it can produce emotional excitement, self-elevation, coarseness... the mashke is
physicality... But today one must take mashke.* (Sefer HaSichos 5687, Purim, §158:3)

He contrasts it with Moshe entering the cloud, "where there was no dross at all."

And the setting matters as much as the substance. The Lubavitcher Rebbe often quoted the Alter
Rebbe: «דאס וואס עס קען אויפטאן א חסידישער פארבריינגען קען מלאך מיכאל ניט אויפטאן»,
*what a Chassidic farbrengen can accomplish, even the angel Michael cannot*
(Igros Kodesh vol. 16, §5921:8, and elsewhere).

**Ours.** That is close to the modern model: a substance opens, the opening carries dross,
and a community with a shared language does the cleaning. It is **not** an endorsement of
psychedelics, which no Chabad source here addresses, and nothing on this site is advice to
use them. Halachic questions about psychedelic therapy belong to a rabbinic authority.

## 9. The beinoni standard: states are not the measure

**Stated.** The Tanya's model person is the *beinoni*, whose heart still fluctuates:

> מִדַּת הַבֵּינוֹנִי, הִיא מִדַּת כָּל אָדָם ... כִּי, הַבֵּינוֹנִי אֵינוֹ מוֹאֵס בְּרָע, שֶׁזֶּהוּ דָּבָר הַמָּסוּר לַלֵּב וְלֹא כָּל הָעִתִּים שָׁווֹת, אֶלָא ״סוּר מֵרַע וַעֲשֵׂה טוֹב״, דְּהַיְינוּ בְּפוֹעַל מַמָּשׁ

*The measure of the beinoni is the measure of every person... for the beinoni does not
loathe evil, which is a matter given over to the heart, and not all times are alike; rather
"turn from evil and do good," in actual deed.* (Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 14:1)

What he can rely on is that "the mind rules the heart by its birth and the nature of its
creation" («כִּי הַמּוֹחַ שַׁלִּיט עַל הַלֵּב ... בְּתוֹלַדְתּוֹ וְטֶבַע יְצִירָתוֹ», Tanya 12:5).
People differ in how they are moved: «כָּל חַד לְפוּם שִׁיעוּרָא דִילֵיהּ [יֵשׁ מִתְפָּעֵל כוּ׳ וְיֵשׁ מִתְפָּעֵל כוּ׳]»,
*each according to his measure; one is moved this way and one that* (Tanya 50:5).

**What this adds.** A "complete mystical experience" is a state. The Tanya's target is a
conduct that holds whatever the state. Tanya 50:5 also anticipates the finding that people
differ in capacity for absorption ([page 3](3-beyond-the-drug.md)).

## 10. Knowing: da'as against the noetic flash

**Stated.**

> וְהַדַּעַת, הוּא מִלְּשׁוֹן ״וְהָאָדָם יָדַע אֶת חַוָּה״, וְהוּא לְשׁוֹן הִתְקַשְּׁרוּת וְהִתְחַבְּרוּת, שֶׁמְּקַשֵּׁר דַּעְתּוֹ בְּקֶשֶׁר אַמִּיץ וְחָזָק מְאֹד, וְיִתְקַע מַחֲשַׁבְתּוֹ בְּחוֹזֶק בִּגְדוּלַּת אֵין־סוֹף בָּרוּךְ־הוּא, וְאֵינוֹ מַסִּיחַ דַּעְתּוֹ

*Da'as is from "Adam knew Chava": a word of binding and joining. He binds his mind with a very
strong and firm bond, fixes his thought firmly on the greatness of Ein Sof, and does not
let his mind turn away.* (Tanya 3:13)

The Rebbe Rashab separates an arousal about "how the thing is good *for me*" (the emotions,
"with self-feeling") from an arousal "only at the good of the thing itself" (the intellect),
and places the deepest union, *dveikus*, beyond arousal altogether
(Hemshech Ayin Beis, part 1, Matos-Masei 5672, §66:4; print ed. part 1, scan p. 94).

**What this adds.** The MEQ's noetic item asks whether the person felt *certain* they met
ultimate reality. Chassidus asks what they *understood*, whether they can hold it, and whether
it is about the thing or about themselves.

## 11. Where Chassidus does not simply correct the science

Honesty requires the other side.

- **Chassidus wants experience.** The Mitteler Rebbe insists the teachings must be fixed "in
  mind and heart *specifically as arousal*" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 2:12). It is not a
  quietism.
- **Grace outranks effort in places.** The index to the Rebbe Rayatz's discourses of 5691
  sets "bittul of something into nothing, through labour" below "essential bittul, from the
  revelation of the light above" (Sefer HaMaamarim 5691, index s.v. ביטול, entry עב, scan
  p. 416; **OCR**, editor's summary). That complicates any simple "earned is better than
  given" contrast with a drug.
- **The lowest divine rung outranks the highest natural one.** Being drawn after G-d "without
  reason," like a child after a father, is placed above the natural soul's best
  (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 22:5–6). Intensity and refinement are not the axis.
- **The science has things Chassidus lacks:** controlled comparison, blinding (when it works),
  base rates, adverse-event counting, and the finding that placebo and expectation alone produce
  psychedelic-like states in some people (4 of 36 met the "complete" criterion after an active
  placebo in Griffiths et al. 2006). Chassidus has a theory of the counterfeit; the lab has
  numbers on it.
