# Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn — The Frierdiker Rebbe (Rayatz)

**English name:** Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn
**Hebrew name:** רבי יוסף יצחק שניאורסאהן
**Honorifics / titles:** the "Frierdiker Rebbe" (Yiddish: "the Previous Rebbe"); the "Rebbe Rayatz" (acronym of **R**abbi **Y**osef **Y**i**tz**chak); the "Sixth Rebbe" of Chabad-Lubavitch; "Admur Maharayatz" (אדמו"ר מוהריי"צ)
**Life years:** 1880–1950 (born 12 Tammuz 5640 in Lubavitch; passed 10 Shevat 5710 in Brooklyn, NY)
**Years of leadership:** 1920–1950 (2 Nissan 5680 – 10 Shevat 5710); 30 years

---

The Frierdiker Rebbe was the sixth Rebbe of the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty, the only son and successor of the fifth Rebbe, Rabbi Sholom DovBer Schneersohn (the Rebbe Rashab), and the father-in-law and immediate predecessor of the seventh Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson. He led Chabad through the Bolshevik persecution of religion in the Soviet Union (where he was arrested and sentenced to death in 1927 before being released and exiled), through interwar Poland and Latvia, and finally to the United States, arriving in 1940 and establishing the movement's headquarters at 770 Eastern Parkway in Brooklyn. His defining literary contributions are twofold: he transformed Chabad's oral tradition into a vast published record — institutionalizing the year-by-year publication of *Sefer HaMaamarim* (chassidic discourses) and *Sefer HaSichos* (talks), and pioneering the genre of chassidic memoir and history (*Sefer HaZichronos*, *Likkutei Dibburim*). He also commissioned the daily-calendar anthology *Hayom Yom*, drawn from his own teachings and compiled by his son-in-law.

## The Corpus at a Glance

The Rayatz's published output runs to roughly fifty-plus volumes and is organized into several distinct genres. The largest body is **chassidus / maamarim** — the formal mystical discourses he delivered as Rebbe, collected as *Sefer HaMaamarim* (Chabad's standard reckoning cites ~26 volumes of maamarim, the year-volumes running 5680–5711, plus 3 *Kuntreisim* volumes of separately-issued pamphlets). A second large body is **sichos / talks** — *Sefer HaSichos* (about 10 year-volumes), informal addresses rich in story and reminiscence, of which *Likkutei Dibburim* is an edited anthology. A third major body is **letters (igros)** — *Igros Kodesh*, 17 volumes of correspondence. A fourth, distinctive to this Rebbe, is **history and memoir** — *Sefer HaZichronos* and numerous historical *kuntreisim*. He authored relatively little freestanding **halacha**; his halachic legacy is chiefly preserved through letters, directives, and educational guides rather than formal codes. A great deal of his original material was in Yiddish (especially the memoirs, talks, and historical writing), with the discourses in Hebrew/loshon hakodesh.

## Complete Works

### Chassidus — Maamarim (Discourses)

**Sefer HaMaamarim — by year** (ספר המאמרים / "Book of Discourses")
- *Type/genre:* Formal chassidic/mystical discourses (maamarim) delivered as Rebbe.
- *Language:* Hebrew (loshon hakodesh); some Yiddish discourses (issued in a parallel Yiddish edition).
- *Extent:* ~26 volumes of maamarim by Chabad's own reckoning. The year-volumes break down (per Wikipedia/Chabadpedia) as 8 vols. for 5680–5689, 1 vol. for 5692–5693, and 15 vols. for 5696–5711 — i.e. the series runs through 5711, not stopping at 5710 — plus 3 volumes of *Kuntreisim* (see next entry). Arranged by Hebrew year (1920–1950). Published by Kehot / Otzar HaChassidim.
- *Description:* The core of his theoretical chassidic legacy; the final discourse, *Basi LeGani* (5710), was published the day he passed.
- **Original online:** Print via Kehot: 5680–5681 vol. — https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HFR-SM80 ; 5692–5693 vol. — https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HFR-SM92 ; 21-vol. set — https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HFR-SM.SB . The full corpus is also browsable on the Chabad Library text portal at https://chabadlibrary.org (book browser under the Maharayatz / מאמרים section; deep per-page URLs there are JS-generated, so navigate from the library root rather than relying on a fixed link).
- **English:** Partial; no complete English translation of the full year-by-year series. *Basi LeGani: Chassidic Discourses* (Kehot) translates the 5710 discourse, and *Lessons in Basi LeGani* (Kehot, trans. Touger) gives text + translation + commentary. *Chassidic Discourses* (2 vols., Kehot, trans. Sholom B. Wineberg — see its own entry below) translates selected maamarim from the *HaKriah VeHaKedushah* journal.

**Sefer HaMaamarim — Kuntreisim** (קונטרסים / "Pamphlets")
- *Type/genre:* Individually issued discourse-booklets later bound together.
- *Language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* 3 collected volumes; originally 100+ separate kuntreisim issued during his lifetime (over a thousand maamarim are catalogued overall, more than a hundred having appeared as standalone booklets).
- *Description:* Discourses (and some letters/essays) distributed as standalone pamphlets, especially in the 1930s–1940s.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org ; print via Kehot, https://store.kehotonline.com
- **English:** A few individual kuntreisim translated as standalone books (see *On the Teachings of Chassidus*, *On Learning Chassidus*, and *The Principles of Education and Guidance* below); no full English set of the *Kuntreisim* series.

**Kuntres Toras HaChassidus** (קונטרס תורת החסידות / "Treatise on the Teaching of Chassidus")
- *Type/genre:* Essay/treatise on the nature of Chabad chassidus. *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* One kuntres.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** Yes — published as a standalone book, *On the Teachings of Chassidus (Kuntres Toras Hachassidus)*, trans. Zalman I. Posner (Kehot). Full text on Chabad.org: https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/147465/jewish/On-the-Teachings-of-Chassidus.htm
  - *Note:* Do not confuse this with *On the Essence of Chassidus* (Hebrew: *Inyana Shel Toras HaChassidus*), which is a discourse by the **seventh** Rebbe (delivered 19 Kislev 5726/1965, English ed. Kehot 1978) — a different work despite the similar name, and not part of the Rayatz's corpus.

### Talks — Sichos

**Sefer HaSichos — by year** (ספר השיחות / "Book of Talks")
- *Type/genre:* Informal talks/addresses, often woven with stories and chassidic history.
- *Language:* Largely Yiddish (published in Hebrew/loshon-hakodesh editions; some Yiddish originals).
- *Extent:* ~10 year-volumes, roughly 5680–5710 (per Wikipedia: 2 vols. covering 5680–5691 and 8 vols. covering 5696–5710). The later years (in America) are the most fully edited.
- *Description:* His talks as Rebbe; the 5700 (1939–1940) volume notably includes a diary of the voyage to America.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org ; print via Kehot, e.g. 5700 English ed. — https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=EFR-SS00
- **English:** Substantial. Multiple year-volumes are translated by Uri Kaploun (Sichos in English) and hosted on Chabad.org as chapter-by-chapter indexed editions (each landing page links out to individual chapter translations rather than being a single full-text page) — 5698 ( https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/4062993 ), 5700 ( https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3150431 ), 5701 ( https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3375856 ), 5703 ( https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/4433252 ), 5705 ( https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/3153012 ). Free.

**Likkutei Dibburim** (לקוטי דבורים / "Collected Talks")
- *Type/genre:* Edited anthology of his talks — teachings, avodah (divine service), and chassidic narrative/history.
- *Language:* Original Yiddish and Hebrew.
- *Extent:* Hebrew original = **3 volumes** (Kehot); Yiddish = **4 volumes** (Kehot); English (Uri Kaploun, Sichos in English) = **5 volumes** (also issued as a 6-volume slipcased set). Originally serialized in the *HaTamim* journal; first collected 5693 (1933).
- *Description:* Talks delivered in Latvia, Poland, and the U.S., 1929–1950; a principal source of Chabad oral history.
- **Original online:** Hebrew 3-vol. set, https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=HFR-LIKUD.S ; Yiddish 4-vol. set, https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=YFR-LIKUD.S ; also browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** Yes — full translation by Uri Kaploun (Sichos in English). Read free online via Chabad.org (e.g., Vol. 3, https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/2719178 ); 5-volume English slipcased set for purchase, https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=EFR-LIKUD.SB

### Letters — Igros

**Igros Kodesh** (אגרות קודש / "Holy Letters")
- *Type/genre:* Collected correspondence (personal and public) — guidance, directives, history, halachic and hashkafic rulings.
- *Language:* Hebrew and Yiddish.
- *Extent:* 17 volumes (Kehot; vols. 1–10 chronological, 11–17 supplemental; later volumes edited by Rabbi Sholom DovBer Levin). Some secondary sources still cite the older figure of 14 volumes; 17 is the current, up-to-date count.
- *Description:* Decades of letters touching on every facet of Jewish life and the rebuilding of Torah institutions.
- **Original online:** Searchable/browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org ; print volumes via Kehot store, https://store.kehotonline.com
- **English:** No complete English translation; selected letters are translated across Chabad.org and Sichos in English anthologies.

### History & Memoir

**Sefer HaZichronos** (ספר הזכרונות / "Book of Memoirs")
- *Type/genre:* Narrative memoir / semi-historical reconstruction of early Chabad and chassidic life.
- *Language:* Yiddish (original); Hebrew edition by Kehot.
- *Extent:* Editions differ by language. The **Yiddish original ran to 3+ volumes** (Vol. 1 published 1947; later Yiddish continuations include a Vol. 3). The **Hebrew Kehot set is 2 volumes**, and the **English *Lubavitcher Rabbi's Memoirs* is likewise 2 volumes**. (Wikipedia's "3 volumes" for the English Memoirs appears to be an error — Kehot sells it as a 2-volume set.) Edited by Dovid Leyb Meckler.
- *Description:* Sweeping reminiscences of the founders of Chassidism and the lineage of the Alter Rebbe's family.
- **Original online:** Hebrew set via Kehot, https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=HFR-ZICH.S ; Yiddish scans on Internet Archive (e.g., Vol. 3, https://archive.org/details/sefer-hazichronos-vol.-3 ).
- **English:** Yes — *Lubavitcher Rabbi's Memoirs*, 2 vols., trans. Nissan Mindel (Kehot), https://store.kehotonline.com/mobile/prodinfo.asp?number=EFR-LUBA.SB

**The Heroic Struggle / Reshimas HaMaasar** (רשימת המאסר / "Account of the Arrest")
- *Type/genre:* Prison diary / first-person account of his 1927 Soviet arrest, sentencing, and liberation.
- *Language:* Yiddish (original).
- *Extent:* One volume.
- *Description:* First-person record of his 5687 (1927) arrest for "counter-revolutionary activity," imprisonment, and release.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org ; English chapters on Chabad.org, https://www.chabad.org/magazine/article_cdo/aid/108319/jewish/The-Heroic-Struggle.htm
- **English:** Yes — *The Heroic Struggle: The Arrest and Liberation of Rabbi Yosef Y. Schneersohn of Lubavitch in Soviet Russia* (Kehot, 1998), trans./adapted by Alter B. Z. Metzger. ISBN 0826604390 (ISBN-13: 9780826604392).

**Historical Kuntreisim** (e.g., *Divrei Yemei…*; *The Tzemach Tzedek and the Haskalah Movement*; founding of Agudas Yisrael; history of the chozrim/discourse-memorizers)
- *Type/genre:* Short historical treatises/pamphlets. *Language:* Hebrew/Yiddish. *Extent:* Multiple short kuntreisim.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** *The Tzemach Tzedek and the Haskalah Movement* (Kehot, trans. Zalman I. Posner) translated; others partial or untranslated.

### Education & Practical Guidance

**Klalei HaChinuch VeHaHadrachah** (כללי החינוך וההדרכה / "Principles of Education and Guidance")
- *Type/genre:* Pedagogical guide/kuntres. *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* One kuntres.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** Yes — *The Principles of Education and Guidance* (Kehot, trans. Y. Eliezer Danzinger). Read on Chabad.org.

**Kuntres Limmud HaChassidus / On Learning Chassidus**
- *Type/genre:* Essay on the study and necessity of chassidus. *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* One kuntres.
- **Original online:** Browsable on the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** Yes — *On Learning Chassidus* (Kehot, trans. Zalman I. Posner). https://store.kehotonline.com

**Moreh Shiur b'Limudei Yom Yom** (מורה שיעור בלימודי יום יום / "Daily Study Guide")
- *Type/genre:* Daily study-schedule guide (covering Chumash, Tehillim, and Tanya). *Language:* Hebrew. *Extent:* One guide.
- *Description:* A fixed daily-portion schedule for set study sessions, listed among the Rayatz's works on Wikipedia.
- *Uncertainty note:* The best-known "Moreh Shiur" today is the Rambam daily-study schedule tied to the **seventh** Rebbe's 1984 takkanah; sources for a distinct, freestanding Rayatz-authored *Moreh Shiur b'Limudei Yom Yom* are thinner. Included on the strength of the Wikipedia attribution, but treat the exact form/edition as unconfirmed.
- **Original online:** See the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** No dedicated English edition.

### Liturgy & Daily Study (source-writings)

**Hayom Yom** (היום יום / "From Day to Day")
- *Type/genre:* Daily-calendar anthology of chassidic aphorisms, customs, and teachings — drawn from the Rayatz's own talks and letters, **compiled by his son-in-law Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson** at the Rayatz's instruction (winter 1942, for the year 5703).
- *Language:* Hebrew/Yiddish.
- *Extent:* One volume, 365+ daily entries.
- *Description:* Though compiled by the future seventh Rebbe, its content is sourced from the Frierdiker Rebbe; included here as the source-writings later compiled into *Hayom Yom*.
- **Original online:** Free on Chabad.org daily study, https://www.chabad.org/dailystudy/hayomyom.asp
- **English:** Yes — multiple translations: *From Day to Day* (Kehot, 1988, bilingual ed. prepared by Yitzchak Meir Kagan) and *Hayom Yom: Tackling Life's Tasks* (Sichos in English; user-friendly translation with stories and commentary, https://shop.sie.org/products/tackling-lifes-tasks-hayom-yom-large-edition ); full English on Chabad.org.

**Tehillim Ohel Yosef Yitzchak** (תהלים אהל יוסף יצחק / Psalms)
- *Type/genre:* The Chabad edition of *Tehillim* (Psalms), published with an appended collection of the Rayatz's letters on the importance and practice of reciting Tehillim. *Language:* Hebrew (Psalms text); appended letters in Hebrew/Yiddish. *Extent:* One volume.
- *Description:* The Kehot edition (first printed 1953) carries the Rayatz's name and concludes with his letters on Tehillim recitation; he founded the worldwide *Chevras Tehillim* (Tehillim Society) in 1942.
- **Original online:** Hebrew text on Chabad.org, https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/6242/jewish/Psalms-Tehillim.htm ; print via Kehot, https://store.kehotonline.com
- **English:** Psalms widely available in English; the appended letters are not issued as a dedicated English book.

**Seder HaSelichos / Selichot (annotated)** — he oversaw editions of liturgical/selichot texts with chassidic customs.
- **Original online:** See the Chabad Library portal, https://chabadlibrary.org
- **English:** No dedicated full English edition of his annotations.

### Maamarim in English Translation (collections)

**Chassidic Discourses** (2 vols.)
- *Type/genre:* English collection of the Rayatz's maamarim. *Language:* English. *Extent:* 2 volumes.
- *Description:* Discourses drawn from the monthly journal *HaKriah VeHaKedushah* (1941–1945), many focused on self-sacrifice, strengthening Judaism, and the coming of Moshiach; with footnotes, glossary, and indices. Translated by Sholom B. Wineberg (Kehot).
- **English:** Free full text on Chabad.org, https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/82895/jewish/Chassidic-Discourses.htm ; print, Vol. 1 — https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=EFR-CHASD1 (ISBN of 2-vol. ed. 9780826604422).

### Works Published Under His Direction (notable)

**Heichaltzu (On Ahavas Yisrael)** (היחלצו)
- *Type/genre:* A maamar of the **fifth** Rebbe (the Rebbe Rashab, delivered Simchas Torah 5659/1898), **reissued under the Rayatz's direction** in 5708/1948 for the fiftieth jubilee of the Tomchei Temimim yeshivah, accompanied by the Rayatz's framing letter.
- *Description:* Listed here for the Rayatz's publishing role, not as his own discourse. (Compare *Kuntres Etz HaChayim*, also by the Rashab and merely reprinted by the Rayatz — correctly excluded as the Rayatz's own work.)
- **English:** *Heichaltzu: On Ahavas Yisrael* (Sichos in English), ISBN 0826604358. Free online, https://www.sichos-in-english.org/books/heichaltzu/foreword.htm

### Periodicals He Founded / Directed
- *HaTamim* (התמים) — Tomchei Temimim journal, 1935–1939 (*Likkutei Dibburim* first appeared here).
- *HaKriah VeHaKedushah* (הקריאה והקדושה) — monthly periodical, 1941–1945, U.S. (source of the *Chassidic Discourses* maamarim).
- **Original online:** Scans on the Chabad Library portal and HebrewBooks; these are containers for much of his writing rather than a single authored "work."

## Scope & Caveats

"Complete" here means the full set of **published, attributed** works as collected by Kehot/Otzar HaChassidim and the Chabad Library; exact volume counts are given as the latest standard editions and a few are marked "approx." because series have been re-edited and re-numbered over time. *Sefer HaMaamarim* is best stated as **~26 volumes of maamarim** (Chabad's own reckoning) with year-volumes running through **5711** (not stopping at 5710), plus 3 *Kuntreisim* volumes; *Sefer HaSichos* runs to ~10 year-volumes; counts vary by edition and by how Kuntreisim and Yiddish volumes are tallied. *Igros Kodesh* stands at **17 volumes** (older sources say 14) and is the kind of corpus that grows as more letters surface. *Likkutei Dibburim* differs by language — **3 Hebrew, 4 Yiddish, 5 English** (also a 6-vol. slipcased English set) — and *Sefer HaZichronos* likewise differs: **3+ Yiddish volumes** versus **2-volume** Hebrew and English (*Lubavitcher Rabbi's Memoirs*) editions.

A significant amount of material remains **uncollected or only partially published**: additional letters, reshimos (private notes/journals), unedited talks, and manuscripts in the Chabad Library archive (including items recovered or contested over the decades). On the **translation** side, English coverage is uneven: *Likkutei Dibburim*, *Hayom Yom*, *Lubavitcher Rabbi's Memoirs*, *The Heroic Struggle*, *Chassidic Discourses*, and several standalone kuntreisim (*On the Teachings of Chassidus*, *On Learning Chassidus*, *The Principles of Education and Guidance*) are fully translated, and most later *Sefer HaSichos* year-volumes are now online in English via Chabad.org / Sichos in English (as chapter-indexed editions) — but the bulk of *Sefer HaMaamarim* and all of *Igros Kodesh* have **no full English translation**.

A note on links: the verified, stable links are the Chabad.org article pages and the Kehot store product pages cited above. The Chabad Library text portal at **chabadlibrary.org** genuinely hosts the full original-language corpus (maamarim, sichos, igros, etc.), but its pages render via JavaScript and its deep per-page URLs are generated dynamically, so it is cited here at the site/section level — navigate from the library root rather than relying on a fixed deep link. The same texts are mirrored on hebrewbooks.org and Otzar HaChassidim. New translations and newly edited Hebrew volumes continue to be released by Kehot and Sichos in English, so this catalog reflects the state of publication as of 2026.
