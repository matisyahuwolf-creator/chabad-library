# Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn — the Rebbe Maharash

**Hebrew name:** רבי שמואל שניאורסאהן (Rabbi Shmuel Schneersohn)
**Honorific / title:** The **Rebbe Maharash** (מהר"ש — acronym for *Moreinu HaRav Shmuel*, "Our Teacher Rabbi Shmuel"); fourth Rebbe (Admur) of Chabad-Lubavitch
**Life:** 2 Iyar 5594 – 13 Tishrei 5643 (29 April 1834 – 14 September 1882)
**Years of leadership:** 5626–5643 (1866–1882), approx. 16 years

---

Rabbi Shmuel was the youngest of the seven sons of Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Lubavitch (the **Tzemach Tzedek**, third Chabad Rebbe), and despite his youth was chosen to succeed his father in the movement's capital of Lubavitch after the Tzemach Tzedek's passing in 5626/1866. He led Chabad for sixteen years until his early death at age 48, and was succeeded by his son Rabbi Sholom Dovber Schneersohn (the **Rashab**, fifth Rebbe). Alongside intense communal advocacy — traveling through Europe and lobbying the Czarist government to halt anti-Jewish decrees and pogroms — his defining literary contribution was a vast body of chassidic discourses (*maamarim*), and above all the innovation of the **hemshech**: an extended series of discourses developing a single theme over weeks or months, a form later brought to its fullest by the Rashab. His teachings are famed for combining great profundity with clarity accessible to scholar and layman alike, and he is popularly associated with the motto **"Lechatchila Ariber"** ("go over [the obstacle] from the outset").

## The Corpus at a Glance

The Rebbe Maharash's surviving body of work is overwhelmingly **chassidus (maamarim)** rather than halacha or a large letter-corpus. The discourses he delivered as Rebbe are collected as **Likkutei Torah — Toras Shmuel**, organized **year by year** in the order the maamarim were delivered (by Torah portion and festival), spanning **5626 through 5642** (1866–1882). (The corpus ends in 5642 rather than 5643 only because he passed on 13 Tishrei — about ten days into 5643 — before delivering a full year's worth of discourses; his leadership itself runs through 5643.) He is estimated to have delivered well over a thousand maamarim. A refined, typeset Kehot edition runs to roughly **26–27 volumes** (the current Kehot complete set is marketed as 26 volumes; Chabad.org cites 27). Beyond the year-by-year series, his published output includes one early posthumous title (**Likkutei Torah L'Sholosh Parshiyos**, his glosses on the Alter Rebbe's *Torah Or*), a single published volume of **letters (Igros Kodesh)** of 70-plus surviving letters and responsa, recorded oral teachings (**sichos**), and several **niggunim** (melodies). Much of his writing reportedly remains in manuscript. There is **no standalone halachic work** comparable to his father's *Tzemach Tzedek* responsa.

## Complete Works

### Chassidus — Maamarim

**Likkutei Torah — Toras Shmuel** (לקוטי תורה — תורת שמואל, "Anthology of Torah — Torah of Shmuel")
- *Type/genre:* Chassidic discourses (maamarim), organized by year of delivery.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (with Aramaic citations).
- *Extent:* The complete chronological corpus of his discourses as Rebbe, years **5626 through 5642** (1866–1882); refined Kehot edition is approx. **26–27 volumes** (several individual years run to two parts). It is the central, defining work.
- *Date authored:* Delivered 5626–5642 (1866–1882).
- *Publication history:* Kehot began issuing the discourses in 1945, the earliest volumes as **facsimiles** of the transcriptions of Reb Shmuel Sofer (the Lubavitch copyist). The Rebbe (Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson) arranged a definitive list of the Maharash's maamarim (1980s), and a refined, newly typeset chronological set was issued by Kehot. Individual years were printed at various dates (e.g., 5626 first printed 5631/1871; 5627–5628 in 5632; 5629 in 5639).
- *Description:* The complete year-by-year collection of the Maharash's chassidic discourses — the core of his legacy.
- **Original online:** Free full Hebrew text at the official Chabad library: http://www.chabadlibrary.org/books/maharash/tsh/index.htm (browse by year, e.g. 5631: https://chabadlibrary.org/books/maharash/tsh/631a/1/index.htm ). Print volumes (paid) from Kehot, e.g. *Toras Shmuel 5626*: https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HRM-TS26
- **English:** No full English translation of the whole corpus. Selected single years/discourses are translated — see *Toras Shmuel 5631 (English–Hebrew)*, *True Existence / Mi Chamocha 5629*, and *Feminine Faith / 5640* below.

**Likkutei Torah L'Sholosh Parshiyos** (לקוטי תורה לשלש פרשיות, "Likkutei Torah on Three Portions"; also rendered *L'Gimmel Parashios*)
- *Type:* Notes / glosses on earlier discourses (not original maamarim in the usual sense).
- *Language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* A short single work — the Maharash's notes/glosses on the **first three parshiyos** of the Alter Rebbe's *Torah Or*.
- *Date:* Published **Vilna, 1884** — posthumously, roughly two years after his passing; the earliest printed Maharash publication.
- *Description:* Distinct from the main *Toras Shmuel* year-by-year series — this is the Maharash's annotations on the opening portions of *Torah Or* rather than his own original discourses. Listed in Wikipedia's bibliography of his works.
- **Original online:** Older edition (rare); content overlaps with material now within the *Torah Or / Likkutei Torah* tradition. No reliable free standalone scan confirmed. **English:** None.

#### Notable hemshechim (discourse-series) within Toras Shmuel
The Maharash pioneered the *hemshech* form. Among his best-known multi-discourse series:
- **Mayim Rabbim 5636** (מים רבים, "Many waters," 1876) — a celebrated hemshech later echoed by the Rashab and the Rebbe (Mayim Rabbim 5738). Found within *Toras Shmuel 5636*.
- **VeKacha 5637** (וככה, "And thus," 1877) — a major series of 46 discourses; newly typeset and published in full by Kehot. Within *Toras Shmuel 5637*.
- *Original online:* Hebrew within the respective year-volumes at http://www.chabadlibrary.org/books/maharash/tsh/index.htm . *English:* No full English translation of these hemshechim.

### English Translations (standalone discourses)

There are at least **three** standalone English Maharash maamarim in print, all bilingual Hebrew/English from Kehot's Chasidic Heritage Series (plus one translated year-volume):

**True Existence — Mi Chamocha 5629** (מי כמוך, "Who is like You")
- *Type:* Single chassidic discourse (maamar), Chasidic Heritage Series.
- *Language:* Hebrew original; English translation.
- *Extent:* One discourse, approx. 98 pp. in the bilingual edition.
- *Date:* Delivered 5629 (1869); English edition 2002.
- *Description:* A foundational discourse on G-d's true existence and the world's contingent existence. The first standalone Maharash discourse published in English (timed to his 120th yahrzeit), translated by Rabbi Yosef Marcus. ISBN 9780826604613.
- **Original online:** Hebrew text within Toras Shmuel 5629 at chabadlibrary.org. Coverage: https://www.lubavitch.com/a-first-in-english-a-chasidic-discourse-composed-in-1869/
- **English:** Published (paid), Kehot, Chasidic Heritage Series. Listing: https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=ERM-TRUE

**Feminine Faith — L'Hovin Inyan Rosh Chodesh 5640**
- *Type:* Single chassidic discourse (maamar), Chasidic Heritage Series.
- *Language:* Hebrew original; English translation.
- *Extent:* One discourse, approx. 104 pp. in the bilingual edition.
- *Date:* Delivered Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan **5640** (1879); English edition 2009.
- *Description:* A discourse on the mystical concept of Rosh Chodesh (the New Moon) — its connection to the feminine, the undivided essence of G-d's reality, and the sin of the Golden Calf. Translated and annotated by Rabbi Shais Taub and Rabbi Avraham D. Vaisfiche. ISBN 9780826607492.
- **English:** Published (paid), Kehot, Chasidic Heritage Series. Listing: https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=ERM-FEMI ; Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/Feminine-Chasidic-Heritage-English-Hebrew/dp/0826607497

**Toras Shmuel — 5631 (English–Hebrew bilingual edition)**
- *Type:* Translated selection of the 5631 (1871) discourses.
- *Language:* Hebrew with facing English translation.
- *Extent:* Covers discourses delivered in 5631; issued in volume(s) (Vol. 2). English edition 2013. ISBN 9780826656223.
- *Description:* The most substantial run of Maharash maamarim available in English.
- **Original online:** Hebrew free at chabadlibrary.org (year 5631).
- **English:** Published (paid), Kehot bilingual edition; also on Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/Toras-Shmuel-5631-English-Hebrew/dp/0826656226

### Talks — Sichos

**Sefer HaSichos — Toras Shmuel / recorded sichos**
- *Type:* Oral teachings and narratives.
- *Extent:* A limited body; the Maharash left far fewer recorded *sichos* than later Rebbeim. A titled collection, **Sefer HaSichos — Toras Shmuel**, appears in Wikipedia's bibliography of his works, but it is a small/derivative gathering rather than a multi-volume *sichos* set; many of his talks and life-details survive only because his grandson, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz), recorded them.
- *Description:* Teachings and reminiscences preserved chiefly within later Chabad literature and the biography below; the standalone *sichos* material is modest.
- **Original (Hebrew/Yiddish):** Embedded in Rayatz/Chabad sources (see *Sefer HaToldos* below). **English:** Via *Sefer HaToldos Admur Maharash* (SIE).

### Letters — Igros

**Igros Kodesh — Admur Maharash** (אגרות קודש, "Holy Letters")
- *Type:* Letters and halachic/communal correspondence.
- *Language:* Hebrew (some Yiddish).
- *Extent:* A single published Kehot volume of **over 70 surviving letters and halachic responsa**.
- *Date:* Letters 1860s–1882; first compiled/published from the Lubavitch Library archive ~1993, with an expanded edition in 2022 (adding 11 letters).
- *Description:* Correspondence on communal defense, chassidic guidance, and practical/halachic matters; documents his lobbying against Czarist decrees. Edited by Rabbi Sholom Ber Levin.
- **Original online:** Print (paid) from Kehot: https://store.kehotonline.com/prodinfo.asp?number=HRM-IK . **English:** No full English translation; excerpts appear in the biography below.

### Biography (about him — not authored by him, included for the serious learner)

**Sefer HaToldos Admur Maharash** (ספר התולדות אדמו"ר מהר"ש, "Book of the Life of the Rebbe Maharash")
- *Type:* Biography, compiled by the Rebbe (Rabbi Menachem M. Schneerson) from the writings/sichos of the Rayatz.
- *Language:* Hebrew original; full English translation (by Shimon Neubort).
- *Description:* The standard life-account, with extensive detail on his family, schedule, advocacy, and the setting of his maamarim — the best companion to the corpus above.
- **Original online (Hebrew/English):** Free full text on Chabad.org: https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/847028/jewish/Sefer-Hatoldos-Admur-Maharash.htm and Sichos in English: https://www.sichos-in-english.org/books/sefer-hatoldos-admur-maharash/
- **English:** Published; free online (above) and in print via SIE: https://shop.sie.org/products/sefer-hatoldos-admur-maharash

### Other

- **Niggunim (melodies):** Several wordless chassidic melodies are attributed to the Rebbe Maharash and are sung in Chabad to this day (not a "text," but part of his transmitted legacy).

## Scope & Caveats

- **What "complete" means here:** This catalog covers the Maharash's *published and publicly catalogued* output. His corpus is dominated by one great series — *Likkutei Torah — Toras Shmuel* — so "complete works" is largely a question of how many years/volumes of that series have been issued.
- **Volume-count uncertainty:** Sources differ. The current Kehot refined complete set is marketed as **26 volumes**; Chabad.org's "The Rebbeim and Their Writings" states **27 volumes**. Treat the figure as **approx. 26–27 volumes**; individual years vary in length (some span two parts). Note: Wikipedia's bibliography line anomalously says "35 volumes" while the same article's prose says twenty-seven — the 35 figure appears to double-count two-part years/sub-volumes and should be treated as unreliable.
- **Date-range note (5642 vs 5643):** His maamarim corpus is dated **5626–5642** while his leadership is given as **5626–5643**. This is not a contradiction: he passed on 13 Tishrei, only about ten days into 5643, so no full year of 5643 discourses exists.
- **Still being released / refined:** Kehot has continued to newly typeset and publish individual years and hemshechim (e.g., the *VeKacha 5637* series). English translation is **fragmentary and ongoing**: only scattered years/discourses exist in English (*True Existence / Mi Chamocha 5629*, *Feminine Faith / 5640*, and *Toras Shmuel 5631*), with no full English rendering of the corpus.
- **Unpublished/uncollected:** A significant amount of the Maharash's writing reportedly remains **in manuscript** and unpublished. His *sichos* survive only in limited quantity, much of it preserved second-hand through the Rayatz and within the biography *Sefer HaToldos Admur Maharash*.
- **Source notes:** The fourth-Rebbe identification, dates, and leadership years are corroborated by Wikipedia, Chabad.org, and Kehot. (One auto-generated search snippet — echoed in places — erroneously called him the "fifth" Rebbe; this is incorrect — he is the **fourth**.)
