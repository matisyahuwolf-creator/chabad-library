# Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson — the Lubavitcher Rebbe

**English name:** Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson
**Hebrew name:** מנחם מענדל שניאורסאהן (הרבי מליובאוויטש)
**Honorific / title:** "the Rebbe"; the seventh Rebbe (Admur / אדמו"ר) of the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty; successor to his father-in-law the Rebbe Rayatz, and known among Chassidim simply as "the Rebbe" (sometimes by the acronym Ramash)
**Life years:** 11 Nissan 5662 – 3 Tammuz 5754 (April 18, 1902 – June 12, 1994)
**Years of leadership:** Accepted the leadership (nesius) on 10 Shevat 5711 (January 17, 1951), one year after his father-in-law's passing on 10 Shevat 5710 (1950); led until his passing on 3 Tammuz 5754 (1994) — roughly 43–44 years.

The Rebbe was the seventh and last rebbe of the Chabad-Lubavitch dynasty, succeeding his father-in-law, Rabbi Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (the Rayatz, sixth Rebbe), whose daughter Chaya Mushka he had married in 1928. Born in Nikolaev, Russia, and educated in Torah and in secular science and engineering in Berlin and Paris, he fled Nazi Europe and arrived in the United States on 28 Sivan 5701 (June 23, 1941). Reluctant to accept leadership, he formally became Rebbe in 1951 and transformed Chabad into the largest and most far-reaching Jewish outreach movement in the world. He and his wife had no children, and he appointed no successor; the movement has continued without an eighth Rebbe. His defining contribution to Chabad literature is one of the largest bodies of Torah scholarship produced by any single figure in the modern era — tens of thousands of edited talks, discourses, and letters spanning halacha, Talmud, Chassidus, Kabbalah, Tanach commentary, and practical guidance, much of it personally edited by him for publication.

## The Corpus at a Glance

The Rebbe's output is unusual in both scale and form: he wrote relatively little as conventional "books," and instead produced an immense oral corpus — delivered at multi-hour farbrengen (Chassidic gatherings) and public addresses — that was transcribed, and, where he reviewed it, personally edited. Estimates run to well over 200 volumes already in print, drawn from an estimated 11,000 hours of public teaching across roughly 1,900 farbrengens over his 42 years of leadership, plus tens of thousands of letters, with major series still being released decades after his passing. (Note: that 11,000-hour figure is the total time the Rebbe spent teaching publicly, *not* hours of recordings — a large portion of the talks, all those said on Shabbat and Yom Tov, was never recorded, since recording is forbidden on those days under Jewish law; those talks were memorized and transcribed afterward by the *chozrim*.) The corpus is best organized into five streams: (1) **Halacha / Talmudic-Maimonidean scholarship** (his Haggadah, Hadranim, Pirkei Avot biurim, and analytic notes); (2) **Chassidus — Maamarim** (formal Chassidic discourses); (3) **Sichos — talks** (his signature genre, ranging from edited masterworks like *Likkutei Sichos* to raw unedited transcripts in *Sichos Kodesh*); (4) **Igros / letters** (correspondence on every conceivable subject); and (5) **Reshimos and other** (private notebooks and specialized compilations). A crucial distinction throughout: **edited (mugah)** texts the Rebbe personally reviewed carry his authority; **unedited (bilti mugah)** transcripts are valuable but provisional. Nearly all original-language texts are freely available on the official **chabadlibrary.org**; English translation covers only a fraction, led by **Sichos in English (sie.org)**, **Kehot**, and **chabad.org**.

## Complete Works

### Halacha & Talmudic / Maimonidean Scholarship

**הגדה של פסח עם לקוטי טעמים ומנהגים — *Haggadah Shel Pesach im Likkutei Ta'amim u'Minhagim*** ("Passover Haggadah with a Collection of Reasons and Customs")
- *Type/genre:* Halachic-textual commentary on the Passover Haggadah (the only full sefer the Rebbe authored and published himself).
- *Original language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* One core work, expanded over decades; the modern Kehot edition is a 2-volume set including later *biurim*, Chabad Passover customs, and the Rebbe's collected talks/letters on the Haggadah.
- *Date:* First published 5706 (1946) in Brooklyn; expanded editions from 5733 (1973) onward; final expanded Kehot edition 5762 (2002).
- *Description:* Concise, encyclopedic notes tracing the order of the Seder and its customs across the full range of Torah — from plain meaning (peshat) to Kabbalah (sod).
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org (search "הגדה של פסח לקוטי טעמים ומנהגים"); also hebrewbooks.org. Paid print: kehot.com / store.kehotonline.com.
- **English:** Full translation exists — *The Haggadah of the Lubavitcher Rebbe* / Kehot 2-vol. edition and Eliyahu Touger's translation; English commentary versions available via sie.org and chabad.org Passover library. Buy via kehot.com.

**תורת מנחם — הדרנים על הרמב"ם והש"ס — *Hadranim al HaRambam v'Shas*** ("Concluding Discourses on Maimonides and the Talmud")
- *Type/genre:* Halachic-Talmudic pilpul (analytic discourse) combining Nigleh (revealed law) and Chassidus.
- *Original language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* One main collected volume (plus related material dispersed in the talk-collections).
- *Date:* Discourses delivered across the nesius, especially tied to the Rambam-study cycle (Takanas HaRambam, from 1984); collected volume first published 1992, republished 2000.
- *Description:* Analytic "siyum" discourses on the concluding passages of Maimonides' Mishneh Torah and of Talmudic tractates.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org and hebrewbooks.org. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** No full English translation of the collected Hadranim; individual hadranim appear in translated talks (e.g., within *Likkutei Sichos* English selections and sie.org material).

**ביאורים לפרקי אבות — *Biurim l'Pirkei Avot*** ("Elucidations of Pirkei Avot / Ethics of the Fathers")
- *Type/genre:* Collected Chassidic-analytic insights on the Mishnaic tractate Pirkei Avot, drawn from the Rebbe's summer farbrengen talks.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (compiled from edited sichos).
- *Extent:* **2 volumes** (Kehot).
- *Date:* Talks delivered over many years (the Rebbe studied Pirkei Avot publicly during the summer Shabbatot); collected and published by Kehot in 1989.
- *Description:* The Rebbe's running elucidation of the teachings of Pirkei Avot, resolving textual difficulties and drawing out their meaning for Divine service.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; hebrewbooks.org. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** Partial — English renderings appear at chabad.org ("Ethics of the Fathers" with the Rebbe's commentary) and in Kehot/sie.org selections; no complete authorized 2-volume English set.

**ביאורים לפירוש רש"י על התורה — *Biurim l'Pirush Rashi al HaTorah*** ("Elucidations of Rashi's Torah Commentary")
- *Type/genre:* Compilation of the Rebbe's "Rashi sichos," organized as a running commentary on Rashi — a discrete published sefer in its own right.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (drawn from edited sichos).
- *Extent:* **5 volumes** — c. 450 full biurim plus nearly 1,000 short notes (end of Vol. V also includes not-yet-edited material on Devarim).
- *Date:* Talks delivered from 1964 over 25+ years; compiled and published by Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos.
- *Description:* The Rebbe's systematic resolution of textual/grammatical/sequential difficulties in Rashi, followed by Chassidic insight. (The underlying "Rashi sichos" also appear within *Likkutei Sichos*; this set gathers them as a dedicated Rashi commentary.)
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; esicha.org (purchase). Also on sefaria.org (the Rebbe's Chumash/Rashi teachings).
- **English:** Partial — *Studies in Rashi* (Kehot) and Eliyahu Touger's translations via Sichos in English render selected Rashi sichos; no complete English set of all five volumes.

**Analytic / Talmudic notes (Chiddushim u'Biurim b'Shas, etc.)**
- *Type/genre:* Talmudic and halachic novellae and analyses.
- *Original language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* Several collected volumes (e.g., *Chiddushim u'Biurim b'Shas*); much also embedded in *Likkutei Sichos* and *Reshimos*.
- *Description:* The Rebbe's lomdishe (analytic) treatments of Talmudic sugyos and halachic questions.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org and hebrewbooks.org.
- **English:** Largely untranslated; selected pieces in translated talk collections.

### Chassidus — Maamarim (Chassidic Discourses)

**ספר המאמרים מלוקט — *Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat*** ("Book of Discourses — Selected/Anthologized")
- *Type/genre:* Formal, personally-edited Chassidic discourses (maamarim) — the authoritative, mugah body of the Rebbe's Chassidus.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (with the classic maamar idiom).
- *Extent:* The canonical set is **6 volumes** (200+ maamarim), published by **Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos** (esicha.org) — this is the set the Rebbe himself edited; Vol. I gathers edited maamarim from 5711–5747, Vols. II–VI follow order of release. (A separate **4-volume** edition by **Vaad Hanachos b'Lahak**, 2002, rearranges the same discourses by calendar month; that Lahak rearrangement was issued **without authorization**, and the Crown Heights Beis Din publicly discouraged purchasing it. Kehot also markets a compact 4-volume set, so retail listings can be confusing — but the authoritative reference is the 6-volume Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos edition.)
- *Date:* Maamarim delivered 5711–5752 (1951–1992); 6-volume series first published Kislev 5747 (1986), completed Elul 5752 (1992).
- *Description:* The Rebbe's edited discourses on Chassidic-mystical themes, the core canon of his Chassidus.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; chabad.org/torah-texts; sefaria.org. Paid print: esicha.org / kehot.com.
- **English:** Partial — selected maamarim translated by Sichos in English and in chabad.org/Kehot collections; no complete English set of all six volumes.

**תורת מנחם — ספר המאמרים (לפי שנים) — *Sefer HaMaamarim, by year*** (e.g., *Sefer HaMaamarim Basi LeGani*, etc.)
- *Type/genre:* Chassidic discourses arranged by year of delivery.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (some originally Yiddish, rendered to Hebrew).
- *Extent:* Multiple yearly volumes within the *Toras Menachem* project; includes themed compilations such as *Sefer HaMaamarim — Basi LeGani* (the annual Yud Shvat discourse series begun by the Rayatz and continued by the Rebbe).
- *Description:* Year-by-year presentation of the maamarim, complementing the topically-selected *Melukat*.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; hebrewbooks.org.
- **English:** Partial; *Basi LeGani* and select discourses translated (Kehot / sie.org). No complete English set.

### Talks — Sichos

**לקוטי שיחות — *Likkutei Sichos*** ("Collected Talks")
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's flagship work — personally-edited essays expounding the weekly Torah portion, festivals, and themes; the authoritative core of his teaching.
- *Original language:* Mixed by volume — Vols. 1–9 Yiddish; 10–14 Hebrew; 15–29 Yiddish; 30–39 Hebrew. (Modern reprints add facing Hebrew translation for the Yiddish volumes.)
- *Extent:* **39 volumes** containing 1,200+ essays (includes the ~800+ "Rashi sichos" delivered over 25 years).
- *Date:* Talks delivered c. 1950s–1992; issued as weekly pamphlets then bound; edited by the Rebbe himself.
- *Description:* Wide-ranging analyses uniting peshat, Talmud, halacha, Kabbalah, Chassidus, and practical directives — considered the heart of the Rebbe's written legacy.
- **Original online:** Free full text at chabadlibrary.org; also chabad.org/torah-texts and sefaria.org. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** Two efforts — (1) older selective translations and topical anthologies (Kehot; *Selections from Likkutei Sichos*, sie.org); (2) a new comprehensive English *Likkutei Sichot* by Sichos in English with Kehot (translation led by Eliyahu Touger), with *Bereishit* (Vol. 1) released 2019 and the multi-volume project ongoing. Browse English renderings at chabad.org ("Likkutei Sichot in English"). No fully-complete 39-volume English set yet.

**ספר השיחות — *Sefer HaSichos* (5747–5752)** ("Book of Talks")
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's edited (mugah) talks of his final active years.
- *Original language:* Hebrew and Yiddish (released alternately in Kfar Chabad magazine [Hebrew] and the Algemeiner Journal [Yiddish]).
- *Extent:* **11 volumes** in the standard Kehot bound set (covering 5747–5752, generally two volumes per year, with indices) — this is the figure chabad.org cites as the count the Rebbe himself edited. (The Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos pamphlet-style edition runs to ~12 booklets, two per year over six years; that is a real count but not the standard/authoritative one.)
- *Date:* Talks of 5747–5752 (1986/87–1992), edited by the Rebbe.
- *Description:* The authoritative edited talks of the Rebbe's last years; among the last material he personally reviewed.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; chabad.org/torah-texts. Paid print: kehot.com / esicha.org.
- **English:** Partial — translated excerpts via Sichos in English (e.g., the *Sichos in English* English-volume series, vols. covering these years); no complete authorized English set of the full *Sefer HaSichos*.

**תורת מנחם — התוועדויות — *Toras Menachem — Hisva'aduyos*** ("The Teaching of Menachem — Farbrengens")
- *Type/genre:* Comprehensive transcribed record of the Rebbe's farbrengens (talks + maamarim) across the nesius.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (transcribed/translated from the originally Yiddish talks).
- *Extent:* Very large and still growing. The edited later years **5742–5752 occupy a set of 43 volumes** (Vaad Hanachos b'Lahak's own count; some Kehot retail listings say 45). The earlier years 5710–5741 are being reconstructed/reissued from transcripts (70+ volumes published, e.g., the 77th volume reaching 5734; the 120th overall volume printed in 5782/2022). Total in print already exceeds 120 volumes.
- *Date:* Farbrengens 5710–5752 (1950–1992); the 5742–5752 portion reflects the Rebbe's own edited sichos/maamarim, the earlier portion is being compiled by Lahak from transcripts.
- *Description:* The fullest day-by-day record of the Rebbe's public Torah, prepared by Vaad Hanachos b'Lahak.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; chabad.org/torah-texts. Paid print: kehot.com / store.kehotonline.com.
- **English:** Largely untranslated as a series; selected farbrengens appear in *Sichos in English* and other Kehot/sie.org translations. No complete English *Toras Menachem* set.

**שיחות קודש — *Sichos Kodesh*** ("Holy Talks")
- *Type/genre:* The early, **unedited (bilti mugah)** transcripts of the Rebbe's farbrengens.
- *Original language:* Primarily Yiddish (original transcripts).
- *Extent:* ~**50 volumes** covering 5710–5741 (1950–1981); a new, expanded and corrected edition is in progress (comparing transcripts against audio and adding previously unpublished talks).
- *Date:* Farbrengens 5710–5741; first published as a set in 5748 (1988) by the choizrim/transcribers.
- *Description:* The raw record of the Rebbe's talks before editing — historically important and fuller in some respects, but provisional in authority.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; sichotkodesh.org. Paid print available (periodic Kehot/affiliate reprints).
- **English:** No systematic English translation; superseded for study purposes by the edited *Toras Menachem* / *Likkutei Sichos*.

### Letters — Igros

**אגרות קודש — *Igros Kodesh*** ("Holy Letters")
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's collected correspondence — Torah, halacha, Chassidus, science, counsel, education, communal affairs, and personal guidance.
- *Original language:* Hebrew and Yiddish (occasionally French and other languages).
- *Extent:* **~34–40 volumes** published to date (counts vary by printing; ~34 is the standard figure, up to ~40 in recent printings, growing as more years are released); estimated ~20,000 letters of public relevance, drawn from a far larger archive.
- *Date:* Letters span the 1920s–1994; the Rebbe instructed publication beginning in 1987; published by Kehot.
- *Description:* One of the richest letter-corpora in Jewish history, addressing virtually every subject of Jewish and human life.
- **Original online:** Free full text at chabadlibrary.org; an interactive "Igrot Kodesh" lookup at igrot.com. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** Partial — anthologies such as *Letters from the Rebbe* (sie.org) and *Igrot Kodesh — Meturgamot* (translated selections, Kehot); thematic collections (e.g., *Healthy in Body, Mind and Spirit*). No complete English translation of the Hebrew/Yiddish set.

**Igros Kodesh — The English Letters** (the Rebbe's English-language correspondence)
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's letters originally written in English (a separate archive from the Hebrew/Yiddish *Igros Kodesh*).
- *Original language:* English (these are originals, not translations).
- *Extent:* Project announced to span ~**50 volumes** and 10,000–15,000 letters over decades; first installment is **Volume II** (1950–1951; reported as ~214 letters in the primary announcement, ~217 in some coverage, of which ~170 previously unpublished). Volume I (the Rayatz-era correspondence) is to come later.
- *Date:* Letters from 1950 onward; project announced on the Rebbe's 32nd yahrtzeit, 3 Tammuz 5786 (June 17, 2026); first volume slated for release after Tishrei 2026.
- *Description:* Long-inaccessible English correspondence preserved as carbon copies by the Rebbe's secretariat, now being edited by Sichos in English and published by Kehot.
- **Original online:** Not yet online (in production); will be published via kehot.com / sie.org.
- **English:** These ARE the English originals (no translation needed).

### Reshimos & Other

**רשימות — *Reshimos*** ("Jottings / Notes")
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's private notebooks — personal Torah notes and novellae, discovered after his passing.
- *Original language:* Hebrew (with characteristic abbreviation).
- *Extent:* Originally issued as ~200+ serialized pamphlets; collected as the standard **3-volume** Kehot edition (plus a separate notebook on Tanya). Later Kehot printings expand the set further — current editions are sold as **6- and 7-volume** sets.
- *Date:* Written c. 5688–5710 (1928–1950), before the nesius.
- *Description:* Concise insights and source-collections across all areas of Torah — Talmud, halacha, Kabbalah, customs, ethics, stories, and avodah — revealing the Rebbe's thought before his public career.
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org; hebrewbooks.org. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** Partial — *Reshimot* selections translated and published in English (Kehot/sie.org, e.g., *The Reshimot* and topical excerpts); no complete English set.

**רשימת היומן — *Reshimas HaYoman*** ("Diary Notes")
- *Type/genre:* The Rebbe's diary/journal entries (teachings, customs, and events observed, especially around the Rayatz).
- *Original language:* Hebrew.
- *Extent:* 1 volume.
- *Date:* Entries largely from the 1930s–1940s; published by Kehot (2006).
- *Description:* First-person records of customs, stories, and teachings, a key source for Chabad minhag.
- **Original online:** Free/searchable at chabadlibrary.org; hebrewbooks.org. Paid print: kehot.com.
- **English:** No full English translation; cited in translated form within other works.

**היום יום — *HaYom Yom*** ("From Day to Day")
- *Type/genre:* Daily anthology of Chassidic aphorisms, customs, and teachings, compiled (and partly authored) by the Rebbe at the Rayatz's request.
- *Original language:* Hebrew/Yiddish.
- *Extent:* 1 volume (a calendar of 12 months, one entry per day) — laid out as the calendar for the Hebrew year 5703.
- *Date:* First published Kislev 5703 (December 1942) as the calendar/daily companion for the year 5703.
- *Description:* A beloved daily-study classic. Although it draws on the teachings of all the Chabad Rebbeim, its entries are taken principally from the talks and letters of the sixth Rebbe (the Rayatz). (Attribution note: this is an anthology compiled by the seventh Rebbe, not an original composition — a point often gotten wrong.)
- **Original online:** Free at chabadlibrary.org and chabad.org/dailystudy (with English).
- **English:** Full English translation freely available on chabad.org ("Hayom Yom") and in Kehot print.

**Topical & translated anthologies** (derivative collections of the Rebbe's teachings)
- *Examples:* *Sichos in English* (50+ English volumes of translated talks, sie.org); *Letters from the Rebbe*; *Healthy in Body, Mind and Spirit*; *Toward a Meaningful Life* (S. Jacobson); *Sound the Great Shofar*; *I Will Write It in Their Hearts*; the *Chumash with the Rebbe's commentary*; *Kehot Chumash*.
- *Description:* Curated, often thematic, English (and Hebrew) compilations distilling the Rebbe's edited talks and letters for general readers.
- **Original online / English:** Many freely readable at chabad.org and sie.org; print via kehot.com.

## Scope & Caveats

"Complete" here means a faithful inventory of the **major published series and their canonical extent**, not a guarantee that every individual pamphlet, talk, or letter is accounted for — the Rebbe's corpus is one of the largest of any single Torah figure and is still being released. Several caveats:

- **Volume counts move.** *Igros Kodesh* (commonly cited at 34, up to ~40 in recent printings), *Reshimos* (3-volume core, now sold in 6- and 7-volume editions), and especially *Toras Menachem — Hisva'aduyos* (120+ volumes and counting, as Lahak reconstructs the early years 5710–5741) grow with new releases; treat all such figures as "approx." and current as of mid-2020s.
- **Watch the publisher, not just the count.** For *Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat*, the authoritative reference is the **6-volume Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos** edition (the set the Rebbe edited); the by-month **4-volume Vaad Hanachos b'Lahak** rearrangement (2002) was issued without authorization and was discouraged by the Crown Heights Beis Din. For *Sefer HaSichos* (5747–5752), the canonical figure is **11 volumes** (Kehot bound set), with a ~12-booklet Vaad L'Hafotzas Sichos edition existing alongside it.
- **Edited vs. unedited matters.** The mugah (Rebbe-edited) texts — *Likkutei Sichos*, *Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat*, the Haggadah, *Sefer HaSichos* 5747–5752 — carry his authority; the *bilti mugah* transcripts (*Sichos Kodesh*, parts of *Toras Menachem*) are provisional and are continually being re-checked against audio.
- **"Hours" is teaching time, not recordings.** The often-cited ~11,000 hours measures the time the Rebbe spent teaching publicly (at roughly 1,900 farbrengens); much of it — all Shabbat and Yom Tov talks — was never recorded and survives only as transcriptions reconstructed from memory by the *chozrim*.
- **Still in progress.** The new comprehensive English *Likkutei Sichot* (multi-decade project), the expanded/corrected *Sichos Kodesh*, the early-years *Toras Menachem*, additional *Igros Kodesh* volumes, and the brand-new *Igros Kodesh — The English Letters* (~50 projected volumes, first volume due after Tishrei 2026) are all ongoing.
- **Unpublished / uncollected.** A large reservoir of letters (well beyond the ~20,000 published), private notes, and audio not yet transcribed or edited remains in the archives of the Library of Agudas Chassidei Chabad and the secretariat; some material is reserved or not yet cleared for publication.
- **Access.** Original-language texts are overwhelmingly **free** on the official **chabadlibrary.org** (and chabad.org/torah-texts, sefaria.org, hebrewbooks.org); **English coverage is partial**, led by Sichos in English (sie.org), Kehot (kehot.com), and chabad.org. Where this catalog says "no full English translation," partial or selected translations may still exist within anthologies.

*Note: The 35-volume* Or HaTorah *is deliberately excluded here — that work belongs to the THIRD Rebbe (the Tzemach Tzedek, also named Menachem Mendel Schneersohn), not to the seventh Rebbe.*
